# Day 4 Trial Digest — April 30, 2026

_Musk v. Altman / OpenAI, N.D. Cal. 4:24-CV-04722-YGR (Hon. Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers)_ _Source: `043026TT.txt` (Volume 4, pages 645-892)_

* * *

## 1\. Summary

Day 4 finished Elon Musk's testimony and put his longtime wealth manager Jared Birchall on the stand. The morning opened with a heated pre-jury conference: Judge Gonzalez Rogers locked Mr. Molo's expert (Prof. Russell) out of any "extinction" or "catastrophe" testimony and admonished both sides. OpenAI counsel Mr. Savitt then resumed a punishing cross of Musk centered on the 2018 Altman term sheet, the "purple box" warning, the 20x and 15x Microsoft redemption multiples, Musk's signing the March 2023 AI-pause letter while xAI was already incorporated, his February 2025 $97.4B bid for OpenAI's assets, and a long list of for-profit Musk companies that Musk himself calls "socially beneficial." Microsoft counsel Mr. Cohen briefly cross-examined Musk on the 2020 GPT-3 license tweet ("OpenAI is essentially captured by Microsoft"). On redirect, Mr. Molo walked Musk back through the founding documents and the 2017 "I've had enough" email to recover the narrative that Musk repeatedly relied on Altman's reassurances to stay on mission. Musk recross sharpened the point that Musk made no further quarterly contributions after 2017 and gave his "last penny" in 2020. Birchall then took the stand for the rest of the day. On direct (Mr. Kry) he authenticated a $38,191,066 contribution summary and explained YC Org as a tax-status "conduit," DAFs at Vanguard and Fidelity, the Tesla gifts, the UBI study donation, the Pioneer Building rent arrangement, and — controversially — the February 2025 xAI-led $97.5B bid. On cross (Mr. Wilson), Birchall conceded he did not know the legalities of DAFs, that Musk Industries (not OpenAI) signed the master lease, that Musk wanted "100 percent of the power and authority" over the Pioneer Building, that Neuralink shared the building rent-free until OpenAI's auditors raised an issue, and that he had no firsthand knowledge that Altman was on "both sides of the table" in the OpenAI restructuring. The day ended with the Court launching an after-jury inquiry into whether plaintiffs had improperly invoked attorney-client privilege during Musk's deposition while now eliciting the very same content at trial through Birchall — to be resolved May 1 at 10:00 a.m.
