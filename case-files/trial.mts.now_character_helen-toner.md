**Helen Toner** is a former member of the **[OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI)** board of directors and the director of strategy at **Georgetown's Center for Security and Emerging Technology (CSET)**. She was one of the four directors — alongside **[Tasha McCauley](https://trial.mts.now/character/tasha-mccauley)**, **[Ilya Sutskever](https://trial.mts.now/character/ilya-sutskever)** and **[Adam D'Angelo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_D'Angelo)** — who voted to **fire [Sam Altman](https://trial.mts.now/character/sam-altman)** as CEO of OpenAI on **November 17, 2023**.

Plaintiffs intend to play her **video deposition** at trial. Defendants have already filed a motion in limine objecting to several of plaintiffs' designations.
