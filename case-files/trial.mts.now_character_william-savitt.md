**William Savitt** is a partner at **Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz** and the **lead trial counsel for the OpenAI defendants** in _Musk v. Altman_. He delivered **OpenAI's opening statement** on Day 1 and conducted the **[Elon Musk](https://trial.mts.now/character/elon-musk)** cross-examination on Days 2–3.

His Wachtell trial team includes **[Mr. Wilson](https://trial.mts.now/character/bradley-wilson)** (who crossed [Birchall](https://trial.mts.now/character/jared-birchall)), **Sarah K. Eddy**, and **Nathaniel Cullerton**, with local counsel **William Frentzen** and **Jordan Eth** of Morrison & Foerster.
