**Steven F. Molo** is a founding partner of **MoloLamken LLP** and the **lead trial counsel for plaintiffs** in _Musk v. Altman_. He delivered **plaintiffs' opening statement** on Day 1 and conducted **[Elon Musk](https://trial.mts.now/character/elon-musk)'s direct examination**.

His MoloLamken trial team includes **[Mr. Kry](https://trial.mts.now/character/robert-kry)**, **Alexandra C. Eynon**, **Jennifer Schubert**, **Sara Tofighbakhsh**, and **Walter Hawes IV**.
