**Lawrence "Larry" Page** is a co-founder of **[Google](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google)** and former CEO of [Alphabet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphabet). He does not appear on any witness list for _Musk v. Altman_, but his name has come up repeatedly in the opening days of trial.

## "Speciesist"

In direct testimony on **Day 1**, [Elon Musk](https://trial.mts.now/character/elon-musk) recounted a dinner conversation with Page — typically dated to roughly **2014–2015** — in which the two argued about the risks of artificial general intelligence. According to Musk, Page dismissed his concern about AI wiping out humanity by accusing him of being a **"speciesist"** for favoring humans over digital intelligences:

> "He called me a speciesist for caring about whether AI replaces or wipes out humans. He said that would be fine." `(042826TT.txt:4828)`

Musk has used the anecdote in many public settings; in this courtroom he offered it as the **founding motivation** for putting his money into [OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI) — to ensure there would be at least one AI lab whose stated mission was the long-term benefit of humans rather than capability for its own sake.
