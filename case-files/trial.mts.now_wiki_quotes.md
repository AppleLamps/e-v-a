# Quotes

The most vivid, memorable, and quotable lines from the _Musk v. Altman_ trial — from every party, witness, and the bench. Verbatim with citations to the daily transcripts in `wiki/extracted/`.

The wiki takes no position on the merits; the trial will decide those. The point of this page is to preserve the courtroom's voice.

Context worth keeping in mind while reading: **[Elon Musk](https://trial.mts.now/character/elon-musk)** co-founded OpenAI in 2015 explicitly as a non-profit AI-safety project, donated roughly **$38 million** in cash, and on the stand has spent days articulating a long-running concern that powerful AI must remain accountable to humanity rather than to a single for-profit. Whether the 2019 capped-profit conversion broke a binding promise is the legal question the jury will answer.

## "Stole a charity"

### Musk's "stole a charity" refrain

The plaintiffs' core legal theory, in Musk's own words. He returns to the phrase across direct, cross, redirect, and recross.

> "It's not OK to steal a charity." — direct `(042826TT.txt:4186)`

> "If the verdict comes up that effectively makes it OK to loot a charity, **the entire foundation of charitable giving in America will be destroyed**." `(042826TT.txt:4204)`

> " **You just — you just can't steal a charity** is what comes down to, and that's what has happened here." `(043026TT.txt:1270)`

> "Essentially, they were trying to steal a charity and we were trying to stop them." — Musk on the $97B bid `(043026TT.txt:1745)`

> " **They stole the charity** and turned it into a for-profit." `(043026TT.txt:2031)`

> " **I would have filed a lawsuit sooner if I thought they had stolen a charity sooner.**" `(042926TT.txt:2552)`

### Three phases & the stolen-car analogy

> "I would just divide this into three phases. Essentially, there was one where I was enthusiastically supported, phase one; phase two where I was a little uncertain; and then phase three where I'm sure they're looting the nonprofit. **We're currently in phase three.**" `(042926TT.txt:2133)`

> "Thinking that someone might steal your car is not the same as if someone has stolen your car... And **I concluded that the car had indeed been stolen, meaning the nonprofit had been looted**, they stole a charity, was my conclusion, in 2023." `(042926TT.txt:2523)`
