**Marc Toberoff** is a Los Angeles entertainment-litigation veteran and the founder of **Toberoff & Associates**. He is plaintiffs' co-counsel in _Musk v. Altman_ and has been **repeatedly singled out by the court** in the first four days of trial.

On **Day 4**, after the court struck **[Birchall](https://trial.mts.now/character/jared-birchall)'s** bid testimony, Judge **[Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers](https://trial.mts.now/character/yvonne-gonzalez-rogers)** specifically traced the offending question back to Toberoff:

> "You shouldn't throw the young lawyers under the bus by not saying anything." `(050126TT.txt:360)`

> "I get to control this courtroom, not you." `(050126TT.txt:498)`
