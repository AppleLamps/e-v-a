**Robert K. Kry** is a partner at **MoloLamken LLP** and a key member of the plaintiffs' trial team. He has argued the bulk of plaintiffs' motions, conducted the **[Jared Birchall](https://trial.mts.now/character/jared-birchall)** direct examination on Day 3, and has been the lawyer to deliver the **end-of-day witness previews** to the court — including the Day 4 disclosure of **[Stuart Russell](https://trial.mts.now/character/stuart-russell)**, **[Greg Brockman](https://trial.mts.now/character/greg-brockman)**, **[Mira Murati](https://trial.mts.now/character/mira-murati)** designations, **[Shivon Zilis](https://trial.mts.now/character/shivon-zilis)**, **[Rosie Campbell](https://trial.mts.now/character/rosie-campbell)**, and **[David Schizer](https://trial.mts.now/character/david-schizer)**.
