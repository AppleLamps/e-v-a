**Mark Elliot Zuckerberg** is the founder, chairman and chief executive officer of **[Meta Platforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meta_Platforms)**. He is not a party or expected witness in _Musk v. Altman_, but two pieces of evidence have put him in the trial record:

- **DX 1156** — a **February 2025 text exchange** between Musk and Zuckerberg discussing whether to bid jointly on **[OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI)'s intellectual property**.
- **DX 1083** — a **letter from Meta to the California Attorney General** in support of [Musk](https://trial.mts.now/character/elon-musk)'s lawsuit; Zuckerberg sent Musk a heads-up that the letter was going out.
