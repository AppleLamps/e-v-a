# Day 5 - May 1, 2026 (Friday)

**Case:** _Musk et al. v. Altman et al._, 4:24-CV-04722 YGR (N.D. Cal. — Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers) **Volume 5 — Pages 893–953 (~60 pages, 2,185 lines)** **Court convened: 9:59 a.m. — adjourned: 11:24 a.m.**

* * *

## 1\. Summary

This was a **half-day session, and entirely outside the jury's presence — no witness testimony was taken at all.** The court used the morning to clean up three big housekeeping matters before the long weekend break: (1) ruling on OpenAI's motion to strike Birchall's testimony about the alleged Musk/xAI bid for OpenAI's nonprofit assets; (2) a section-by-section walk-through of the proposed final jury instructions (charitable trust, unjust enrichment / restitution, aiding-and-abetting, statute of limitations, laches); and (3) trial logistics — the Wu (Microsoft 30(b)(6)) declaration, Murati deposition designations, and the witness lineup for next week.

The most consequential moment was Judge Gonzalez Rogers granting the motion to strike Birchall's "bid" testimony, scolding plaintiffs' lead trial counsel Marc Toberoff from the bench for letting junior lawyers (Hawes and Kry) "take the bus" for a question Toberoff himself had pushed. On the law, the judge sided with defendants on the California Bus. & Prof. Code §17510.6/.8 solicitation issue (membership exception controls under _Cohen v. Kabbalah Centre_), and pressed Kry hard on plaintiffs' attempt to disentangle "unjust enrichment" from a "claim for restitution" — a fight that previews the post-jury equitable-remedies phase. Judge also previewed that audio-only public livestreaming of the trial will likely begin Monday once Local Civil Rule 77-3 is in force. The session ended with the witness lineup for next week being laid out: **Stuart Russell and Greg Brockman on Monday; Brockman likely continuing into Tuesday, then video depositions, then Shivon Zilis (possibly Tuesday), Rosie Campbell, and nonprofit-law expert Dean Schizer. Plaintiffs confirmed they will NOT call Sam Altman in their case-in-chief — they'll examine him during OpenAI's case. Microsoft's Satya Nadella will be examined during Microsoft's case under a Rule 50(a) standstill agreement.**

Neither Birchall nor Zilis took the stand today.

## 5\. Notable judicial / procedural moments

### Granting the motion to strike Birchall's "bid" testimony

The court issued a detailed bench ruling, listing on the record exactly what she had reviewed:

> "I'VE RE REVIEWED THE CROSS-EXAMINATION BY MR. SAVITT OF MR. MUSK AT PAGES 687 TO 692 OF THE TRANSCRIPT; THE DIRECT BY MR. KRY WHICH RE OPENED THE ISSUE AND WHICH IS THE SUBJECT OF THE MOTION OF MR. BIRCHALL. THAT'S AT 783 TO 787. THE CROSS-EXAMINATION OF MR. BIRCHALL BY MR. WILSON AT PAGE 784 AND 785, AND THEN AGAIN AT 862, THE REDIRECT." — THE COURT (050126TT.txt:281-286)

> "THE MOTION TO STRIKE IS GRANTED. I WILL SAY THAT THIS WAS IN SOME WAYS A WASTE OF TIME." — THE COURT (050126TT.txt:294-295)

> "I HAVE SOME CONCERNS ABOUT WHETHER THIS WAS A LITIGATION TACTIC. IF IT WAS A LITIGATION TACTIC, THEN PERHAPS IT'S COVERED BY A PRIVILEGE. IF IT WASN'T A LITIGATION TACTIC, THEN IT'S A BUSINESS DEAL, AND IT'S NOT COVERED." — THE COURT (050126TT.txt:328-331)

> "TO ACTIVELY REOPEN WITH A PERSON WHO HAS NO PERSONAL KNOWLEDGE AND HAS OTHERWISE BEEN INSTRUCTED NOT TO SAY ANYTHING, WE DIDN'T NEED IT. IT WAS A TOTAL WASTE." — THE COURT (050126TT.txt:352-354)

### Dressing down Marc Toberoff

> "AND, MR. TOBEROFF, YOU'RE A SENIOR LAWYER. I WAS UP HERE TRYING TO GET ANSWERS. YOU SHOULD HAVE GOTTEN UP AND SAID 'I WAS THE ONE WHO SENT HIM THE QUESTIONS.'" — THE COURT (050126TT.txt:356-358)

> "YOU SHOULDN'T THROW THE YOUNG LAWYERS UNDER THE BUS BY NOT SAYING ANYTHING. THAT'S NOT APPROPRIATE. IT'S JUST NOT APPROPRIATE." — THE COURT (050126TT.txt:360-362)

> "I GET TO CONTROL THIS COURTROOM, NOT YOU." — THE COURT (050126TT.txt:498-503)

> "MR. MOLO, I'M LOOKING TO YOU AS LEAD COUNSEL FOR THAT TEAM. SO THE BUCK STOPS WITH YOU. ALL RIGHT?" — THE COURT (050126TT.txt:539-540)

### Solicitation / membership exception ruling

> "UNDER COHEN VERSUS KABBALAH — THAT'S K-A-B-B-A-L-A-H CENTRE, 35 CAL.APP. 5TH 13 AT 23, 2019 — THAT ACCORDING TO THAT PARTICULAR COURT OF APPEAL DECISION, THEY HAVE INDICATED THAT 17510.6 TRUMPS 17510.8; AND, THEREFORE, IF YOU ARE A MEMBER, THE SOLICITATION APPROACH IS NOT AVAILABLE TO YOU." — THE COURT (050126TT.txt:604-609)

The court rejected Mr. Kry's argument that pre-2018 solicitations made _before_ OpenAI was even formed could trigger the exception:

> "WELL, THEN WAS THERE ANY CHARITY?" — THE COURT (050126TT.txt:640)

### Pushing back on Kry's restitution / unjust enrichment theory

This is the core preview of the equitable-remedies phase. The judge made clear plaintiffs cannot "disentangle" themselves from restitution doctrine:

> "THE SUPREME COURT OF CALIFORNIA HAS REPEATEDLY SAID THERE IS NO SEPARATE CAUSE OF ACTION FOR UNJUST ENRICHMENT. IT IS A THEORY OF RESTITUTION THAT RESIDES IN THE HEART OF QUASI CONTRACT AND UNJUST ENRICHMENT." — THE COURT (050126TT.txt:1245-1248)

> "BUT WHAT YOU WANT TO DO IS YOU WANT TO DISASSOCIATE YOURSELF FROM THE FUNDAMENTAL CLAIM OF RESTITUTION. THAT'S WHAT YOU WANT TO DO." — THE COURT (050126TT.txt:1412-1414)

> "THIS IS NOT A CLAIM FOR QUANTUM MERUIT. I REJECTED DEFENDANTS' APPROACH TO THROW IT OUT ON THAT BASIS FOR SUMMARY JUDGMENT. SO IT'S A CLAIM FOR RESTITUTION UNDER A THEORY OF UNJUST ENRICHMENT, AND YOU CANNOT DISENTANGLE YOURSELF FROM THAT." — THE COURT (050126TT.txt:1417-1425)

> "THERE IS NO SEPARATE CAUSE OF ACTION FOR UNJUST ENRICHMENT. THE ONLY THING YOU FIND IN CALIFORNIA LAW IS A CLAIM OF RESTITUTION UNDER A THEORY OF UNJUST ENRICHMENT." — THE COURT (050126TT.txt:1473-1475)

> "MR. MUSK'S NOTION THAT SOMEHOW HE WANTS SOMETHING DIFFERENT, I DON'T KNOW THAT HE CAN DO THAT BECAUSE IT'S A CLAIM FOR RESTITUTION. AND THE ELEMENTS OF RESTITUTION SAY THAT IT GOES BACK TO THE PLAINTIFF." — THE COURT (050126TT.txt:1446-1449)

Plaintiffs floated a workaround:

> "MR. MUSK WOULD MAKE AN IRREVOCABLE INSTRUCTION TO THE FOUNDATION, OR THE DONOR ADVISED FUNDS, THAT THE RECOVERY WOULD BE DONATED BACK TO THE FOR-PROFIT CHARITY." — MR. KRY (050126TT.txt:1502-1505)

To which Ms. Eddy responded: "THAT WOULD NOT SOLVE THE PROBLEM." (050126TT.txt:1509)
