**Samuel Harris "Sam" Altman** is the CEO and a co-founder of **[OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI)** and the named defendant whose surname appears in the case caption. Plaintiffs' counsel **[Mr. Kry](https://trial.mts.now/character/robert-kry)** confirmed at the close of Day 4 that **Altman will not be called in plaintiffs' case-in-chief** — he will instead be examined during OpenAI's defense case. `(050126TT.txt:2037)`

A former president of [Y Combinator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Y_Combinator), Altman co-founded OpenAI with [Elon Musk](https://trial.mts.now/character/elon-musk), [Greg Brockman](https://trial.mts.now/character/greg-brockman), [Ilya Sutskever](https://trial.mts.now/character/ilya-sutskever) and others in **December 2015**. After Musk's 2018 departure he became CEO of the new for-profit affiliate created in 2019 and led the negotiation of OpenAI's partnership with [Microsoft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft). He was briefly **fired by the OpenAI board in November 2023** — including by directors [Helen Toner](https://trial.mts.now/character/helen-toner) and [Tasha McCauley](https://trial.mts.now/character/tasha-mccauley) — and reinstated five days later. Both directors are expected to testify by video deposition in this trial.

## Trial role

Altman is one of two individual defendants (the other being **[Greg Brockman](https://trial.mts.now/character/greg-brockman)**). Although central to the case, he has not yet appeared in person; the courtroom has heard his name dozens of times in the first four days through emails, exhibit excerpts, and witness testimony.

The defense case is expected to begin **after** plaintiffs rest. Altman's examination is anticipated to address:

- the founding negotiations and Musk's 2017–2018 demands for majority control;
- the rationale for the **2019 capped-profit** restructure;
- the November 2023 board crisis and his reinstatement;
- and OpenAI's relationship with [Microsoft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft).
