**Ilya Sutskever** is the **co-founder** and former **chief scientist** of **[OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI)**. Trained under [Geoffrey Hinton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffrey_Hinton) in Toronto, Sutskever was a research scientist at **[Google](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google) Brain** when he agreed in late 2015 to leave for the new nonprofit lab — a recruitment **[Elon Musk](https://trial.mts.now/character/elon-musk)** described from the witness stand as the move that "made OpenAI possible." `(042826TT.txt:4828)`

He was deeply involved in the **November 2023 board crisis** that briefly removed [Sam Altman](https://trial.mts.now/character/sam-altman) as CEO, and left OpenAI in May 2024 to co-found **Safe Superintelligence Inc.**

## Trial role

Sutskever has been referenced repeatedly in evidence — particularly in the founding-era email chain and in [Larry Page](https://trial.mts.now/character/larry-page)–era recruiting anecdotes — but his live-testimony status remains unclear. Plaintiffs' Day 4 witness preview did not list him.
