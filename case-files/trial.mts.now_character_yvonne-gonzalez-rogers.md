**The Honorable Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers** is the United States District Judge presiding over _Musk v. Altman_. Appointed to the bench in 2011, she sits in the **Northern District of California** in Oakland and is best known publicly for presiding over _Epic Games v. Apple_.

In the first four trial days she has not been afraid to admonish counsel on either side:

- She personally interrogated **[Elon Musk](https://trial.mts.now/character/elon-musk)** about his **pre-trial tweets** concerning this case.
- She **scolded plaintiffs' counsel [Marc Toberoff](https://trial.mts.now/character/marc-toberoff)** from the bench, including: _"You shouldn't throw the young lawyers under the bus by not saying anything."_`(050126TT.txt:360)` and _"I get to control this courtroom, not you."_`(050126TT.txt:498)`
- She openly remarked on **[Musk](https://trial.mts.now/character/elon-musk)'s [xAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/xAI)** as "a company that is in the exact space" he warns of: _"I suspect that there are plenty of people who don't want to put the future of humanity in Mr. Musk's hands."_`(043026TT.txt:801)`
- On **Day 4** she **struck** the bid testimony of plaintiffs' second witness, **[Jared Birchall](https://trial.mts.now/character/jared-birchall)**, finding "blatant attempts to evade" prior orders.
