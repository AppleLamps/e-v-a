**Elon Reeve Musk** is the lead plaintiff in _Musk v. Altman_. The CEO of [Tesla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesla), [SpaceX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpaceX), [X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X_(Twitter)), [Neuralink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuralink) and (since 2023) the founder of competing AI lab **[xAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/xAI)**, Musk co-founded **[OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI)** in December 2015 alongside [Sam Altman](https://trial.mts.now/character/sam-altman), [Greg Brockman](https://trial.mts.now/character/greg-brockman), [Ilya Sutskever](https://trial.mts.now/character/ilya-sutskever) and others. He served on the OpenAI board until **February 2018** and donated roughly **$38 million** to the original 501(c)(3) over the course of his involvement.

His stated motivation, repeated across days of testimony, was AI safety: he co-founded OpenAI as a non-profit because he believed advanced AI needed to be developed openly and accountably to humanity rather than controlled by any single for-profit company. Beyond his $38M in cash, he provided early compute, helped recruit the founding technical leadership (including [Ilya Sutskever](https://trial.mts.now/character/ilya-sutskever) from [Google](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google)), and lent the project the credibility of his name during its first three years.

He filed this lawsuit in 2024 alleging that OpenAI's 2019 reorganization into a "capped-profit" entity, and the **$1 billion + later commitments** from [Microsoft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft) that the new structure unlocked, breached the agreement under which he funded the nonprofit. He seeks rescission of the for-profit transactions and disgorgement of OpenAI's gains.

## Trial role

Musk took the stand on **Day 1 (April 28, 2026)**, completed direct examination on **Day 2**, and was crossed by **[William Savitt](https://trial.mts.now/character/william-savitt)** of Wachtell on **Day 2** and **Day 3**. He was held subject to recall.

His testimony covered:

- the **founding history** of OpenAI, including his recruitment of [Ilya Sutskever](https://trial.mts.now/character/ilya-sutskever) from [Google](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google);
- the **[Page](https://trial.mts.now/character/larry-page) "speciesist" anecdote** he says motivated the entire project;
- the **2017–2018 negotiations** over taking OpenAI for-profit (Musk asked for majority control; the founders refused);
- his **2024 founding of xAI** and the **February 2025** $97.4 billion bid he led for OpenAI's nonprofit assets;
- and his pre-trial **tweets** about the case, which the court interrogated him about directly.

## Notable on the stand

> "That was the whole basis for my donations, why I gave $38 million and my name and helped recruit the people, and taught them everything I know about how to make a company successful. **It was all for the benefit of the charity.**" — Musk, redirect, on why he funded OpenAI. `(043026TT.txt:2974)`

> "I could have started it as a for-profit, and I chose not to. I specifically chose to make it something for the benefit of all humanity." `(042826TT.txt:5442)`

> "Whoever is in charge of AI, are they trustworthy? And if you have someone who is not trustworthy in charge of AI, **I think it's very dangerous to the whole world.**" — Musk on why governance of AI matters. `(042826TT.txt:5226)`

> "I was a fool." — Musk on having donated $38 million in good faith without securing written terms. `(042826TT.txt:5012)`
