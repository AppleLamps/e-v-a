**Satya Narayana Nadella** is the **chairman and chief executive officer** of **[Microsoft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft)**. Microsoft is a defendant in _Musk v. Altman_; Nadella personally negotiated the company's escalating partnership with **[OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI)** — the **2019 $1 billion** investment, the **2020 GPT-3 exclusive license** (DX 1293), and the **2023 commitments** that plaintiffs frame as "capturing" the lab.

Plaintiffs' counsel **[Mr. Kry](https://trial.mts.now/character/robert-kry)** disclosed at the end of **Day 4** that Nadella will not appear during plaintiffs' case-in-chief; instead, he will be examined live during **Microsoft's defense case** under a stipulation that tolls Microsoft's Rule 50(a) clock so plaintiffs are not penalized for that scheduling. `(050126TT.txt:2044)`
