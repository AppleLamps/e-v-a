**Gregory "Greg" Brockman** is the **president** and a **co-founder** of **[OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI)** and one of two individual defendants in _Musk v. Altman_ (the other being CEO **[Sam Altman](https://trial.mts.now/character/sam-altman)**). A former CTO of [Stripe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stripe), Brockman left to help launch OpenAI in **December 2015** and has been at the company in a senior executive capacity ever since.

Plaintiffs' counsel **[Mr. Kry](https://trial.mts.now/character/robert-kry)** disclosed at the end of **Day 4** that Brockman will be the second live witness in plaintiffs' case-in-chief, taking the stand **Monday May 4 (Day 5)** and continuing into Day 6. `(050126TT.txt:2003)` His testimony is expected to focus on the founding period and the email record from 2015–2018 in which the founders debated whether to take the lab for-profit.

## Trial role

Brockman is named as an individual defendant. Unlike [Sam Altman](https://trial.mts.now/character/sam-altman), plaintiffs are calling him directly in their own case rather than waiting to examine him during the defense case.
