**Gregory "Greg" Brockman** is the **president** and a **co-founder** of **[OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI)** and one of two individual defendants in _Musk v. Altman_ (the other being CEO **[Sam Altman](https://trial.mts.now/character/sam-altman)**). A former CTO of [Stripe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stripe), Brockman left to help launch OpenAI in **December 2015** and has been at the company in a senior executive capacity ever since.

## Trial role

Brockman was the **second live witness** in plaintiffs' case-in-chief, called directly rather than waiting to be examined during the defense case. He took the stand on **Day 5 (May 1, 2026 — partial)**, returned for **Day 6 (May 4)** and a full **Day 7 (May 5)**, and was **released after Day 7 redirect** — five sessions of testimony in total.

The throughline of his testimony was a single document: the **personal laptop journal** he has kept since **2010**. **460 pages** were produced in discovery; ~100 of those cover just the **August–September 2017 negotiation window**. Plaintiffs put four entries in evidence as **PX 151, 154, 161, 163**; the defense added a fifth, **DX 1252**, on Day 7. See [Brockman Journal](https://trial.mts.now/wiki/brockman-journal) for every passage put to him on the stand and the parties' interpretive fight over them.

### What landed

- **Day 6 cross (Mr. Kry):** unspooled the journal — " **This is the only chance we have to get out from under Elon … take me to $1,000,000,000**" (PX 151); " **morally bankrupt**" / " **if three months later we're doing a B Corp, that it was a lie**" (PX 161); " **flip to a full profit. Making money for us sounds great**" (PX 163). Also: the **$10 million** undisclosed family-office grant from Sam Altman (PX 350); the undisclosed **Cerebras** investment of March 17, 2017; the never-paid 2015 **$100,000** donation pledge to Marissa Mayer; ownership stake **~$30 billion** acquired in 2018 having paid **$0**.
- **Day 6 redirect (Mr. Savitt):** "Did you ever **commit to Elon Musk that OpenAI would stay a nonprofit**?" — " **No.**" _(5/4/2026 Testimony @ ~12:11 PT)_
- **Day 7 direct (Wachtell):** chronological walk through 2017 — Karpathy poach, Dota victory, the August equity blow-up (Musk's 62.5% demand, the Aug 29 storm-out), the Sept 12 phone call (PX 154 — " **must tell the story and not lose moral high ground**"), the Tesla-merger pitch (" **we should merge OpenAI into Tesla. … It would have to be secret.**"), Musk's all-hands resignation (" **He said he would not work on safety. … If the sheep are dictating safety and the wolves are not, then there's no purpose.**"), the 2018 IP transfer to OpenAI LP, GPT-1 → GPT-4, the November 2023 board firing, his own quit hours later.
- **Day 7 recross (Mr. Molo):** " **It's just an after-the-fact manufactured excuse for the very honest emotion that you expressed in the journal at the time.**" _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~14:08 PT)_ The " **$30 billion**" set piece: " **Couldn't you have given the nonprofit some money to carry out its nonprofit activities**?" → " **I did not donate cash to the nonprofit. No.**"
- **Day 7 redirect (Mr. Gerrada):** introduced **DX 1252** (Nov 5, 2017 — " **Real decision is fire Elon**" / " **So we seem converged on the Fire Elon route**") admitted as a **prior consistent statement** under FRE 801(d)(1)(B) over Mr. Molo's hearsay objection.

### The interpretive fight in one paragraph

Brockman's defense reading is that " **morally bankrupt**" referred to _voting to remove Musk from the OpenAI board and then creating a B Corp without him_ — not to the for-profit conversion as such. He testified the " **only chance to get out from under Elon**" line was about saying no to Musk's unilateral-control demand, after which Musk had said he would quit and they would build the for-profit alone; the " **$1,000,000,000**" line was a private mental ceiling above which the with-Musk-vs-without-Musk choice would be financially indifferent to him. Plaintiffs' reading is that the journal is a **contemporaneous record of intent** to push Musk out and convert OpenAI to a for-profit while publicly maintaining commitment to the nonprofit.

### On the journal itself

> " **Very chain of thought**… stream of consciousness… a lot of things that are self contradictory… trying to test out — do I believe this? Something someone else said that I kinda write down to see how it feels." _(5/4/2026 Testimony @ ~11:09 PT)_

> " **It's very painful. Very deeply personal writings that were never meant for the world to see, but there's nothing in there I'm ashamed of.**" _(5/4/2026 Testimony @ ~11:10 PT)_

> " **I've honestly never really been certain what I'm being sued for.**" _(5/4/2026 Testimony @ ~12:04 PT)_
