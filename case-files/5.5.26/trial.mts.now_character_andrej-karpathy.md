**Andrej Karpathy** is a Slovak-Canadian AI researcher who served as **director of AI** at [Tesla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesla) (2017–2022) and earlier as a research scientist at [OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI) (2015–2017). In 2024 he founded **Eureka Labs**.

OpenAI's defense has put **DX 773** in front of the jury — the **2017 communications** in which **[Elon Musk](https://trial.mts.now/character/elon-musk)**, then on OpenAI's board, **recruited Karpathy out of OpenAI to lead Tesla's Autopilot AI**. Defendants use it to undermine plaintiffs' "Musk acted purely for the benefit of OpenAI" narrative.
