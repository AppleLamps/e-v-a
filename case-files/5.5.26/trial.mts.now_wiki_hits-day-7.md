# The Hits from Day 7

\*\*Day 7 of _Musk v. Altman_ — Tuesday, May 5, 2026.\*\* Second day of the public audio livestream under Local Civil Rule 77‑3.

> **→ [Read the full Day 7 digest](https://trial.mts.now/day/7)** — speaker‑attributed transcript of every line of testimony, witness‑by‑witness breakdown, every exhibit, every notable judicial moment.

## In two paragraphs

**Day 7 was Greg Brockman's full day on the stand** — a chronological replay of 2017 in the morning (Wachtell direct), Mr. Molo's cross built around the journal in the afternoon, and a defense redirect that introduced **DX 1252** — Brockman's Nov 5, 2017 entry " **Real decision is fire Elon**" / " **So we seem converged on the Fire Elon route**" — admitted as a _prior consistent statement_ to rebut Mr. Molo's express charge that the morning's "morally-bankrupt-meant-removing-Elon-from-the-board" gloss was "an after-the-fact manufactured excuse for the very honest emotion that you expressed in the journal at the time." See [Brockman Journal](https://trial.mts.now/wiki/brockman-journal) for every entry the parties fought over.

**The dollar figures finally landed in evidence** when the defense played the **Robert Wu** OpenAI 30(b)(6) deposition: total target redemption across all LPs is **roughly $250 billion** before the nonprofit's residual is paid, Microsoft's 2023 round was a **$60B / 6×** target, the target redemption escalates **20% per year starting 2025**, and Microsoft's 2023 JDCA license covers " **all IP and technology … excluding only AGI**." The **Watershed MOU** — non-binding — would let Microsoft reach **product IP after a third-party-panel determination of AGI**. Wu also confirmed that as early as **April–May 2024**, OpenAI's " **mission strategy committee**" began considering **inverting the LLC waterfall into uncapped corporate stock**. Miss Puckett briefly authenticated OpenAI's 2018 and 2019 Forms 990; jury sent home; **Shivon Zilis** is up next.

> " **This is the only chance we have to get out from Elon.** … We truly have a chance to make this happen financially. **What will it take me to 1,000,000,000? Woo.**" — PX 151, Aug 21, 2017 _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~09:54 PT)_

> "Elon. Coming weeks, top priority. … **Must tell the story and not lose moral high ground. Absolutely vital.**" — PX 154, Brockman's notes of a Sept 12, 2017 phone call, quoted as Musk's words _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~09:22 PT and ~12:33 PT)_

> " **Real decision is fire Elon.** … So we seem converged on the **Fire Elon route**." — DX 1252, Nov 5, 2017 — admitted as a prior consistent statement _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~13:05 PT)_

> " **Cannot say that we are committed to the nonprofit.** Don't wanna say that we're committed. If three months later, we're doing B Corp, **then it was a lie.**" — PX 161, Nov 6, 2017 _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~10:35 PT)_

> "Our biggest tool is the moral high ground. To retain this, we must try our best to remain a nonprofit. **AI is going to shake up the fabric of society, and our fiduciary duty should be to humanity.**" — Brockman → Musk, Jan 31, 2020 (PX 164 — an _email_, not a journal entry, included with the journal sequence on Day 7) _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~12:36 PT)_

### Brockman's narration of Musk

> "He said, **don't worry. I've got the perfect idea. It will actually work. Which is Tesla.** … He said that we should **merge OpenAI into Tesla**. … He said no. **It would have to be secret. That the shareholders wouldn't like it.**" _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~11:52–11:54 PT)_

> "He just sat quiet for several minutes just thinking. … **He said, I decline.** He stood up, and he kinda stormed around the table. … **I truly thought he was going to physically attack me.** … He turned around, and he said, **when will you be departing OpenAI?** … And he said, **I will withhold funding until you decide what you're going to do.**" — Brockman on the Aug 29, 2017 meeting _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~12:11 PT)_

> " **He said he would not work on safety.** He said that the most important thing was to catch up and exceed DeepMind. **And so if the sheep are dictating safety and the wolves are not, then there's no purpose.**" — Brockman, on Musk's all-hands resignation speech _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~12:43 PT)_

> Q: "What was your reaction?" — A: " **Relief.**" _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~12:44 PT)_

### Mr. Molo's cross — the punchline beats

> Mr. Molo: " **It's just an after-the-fact manufactured excuse for the very honest emotion that you expressed in the journal at the time.**" _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~14:08 PT)_

> Mr. Molo: "Before this trial, did you think it was just okay to take the assets of the nonprofit and create a for-profit for which **you would put in no money and walk away with $30,000,000,000**?" _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~14:14 PT)_

> Mr. Molo: "Well, you had **$30 billion**. **Couldn't you have given the nonprofit some money to carry out its nonprofit activities** if the mission was so important to you?" — A: " **I did not donate cash to the nonprofit. No.**" _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~14:24 PT)_

> Mr. Molo, on the post-Nov 2025 nonprofit programming push: " **That activity only occurred after November of this past year.** … And that was **so that you could say in this trial that the nonprofit was actually doing something** as opposed to just holding the money that it had. Right?" _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~14:26 PT)_

> Mr. Molo: "\[Mr. Musk\] was **instrumental** in founding and kickstarting and getting off the ground OpenAI, was he?" — A: " **I don't know if I would use that word.**" _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~14:32 PT)_

### From the bench

> The Court, after Brockman tried to narrate his way through a yes/no on the Sept 20 ultimatum: " **Real simple, Mr. Brockman. He asked you, did you see it? You see it? Yes. That's the answer. Focus on the question.**" _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~14:21 PT)_

> The Court, taking over the IP-transfer question Mr. Molo couldn't get a clean answer on: "My question is the intellectual property that was the property of the nonprofit. **You transferred to the for-profit and that was the foundation of the advances that you described in that green and black chart.**" — A: " **There's a lot of pieces to that question, so I'm uncertain whether to say yes or no.**" _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~14:23 PT)_

> The Court, ruling DX 1252 in over Mr. Molo's hearsay objection: "I'll allow it in. … **I'll just have the witness read the key.**" _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~14:37 PT)_

> The Court, declining Mr. Molo's sealed motion to limit the livestream for sensitive personal-history material: "This information is somewhat old. **Came out in the book that Mr. Musk himself authorized. Mr. Isaacson** — Mr. Brockman has testified about it. So I don't think they're the basis for cutting off the transmission." _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~13:33 PT)_

### From the Robert Wu deposition (OpenAI 30(b)(6))

> Wu: " **Back-of-the-napkin math here, that the total target redemption amounts for all of the limited partners, including the nonprofit, and Microsoft, rounded to $250,000,000,000.** And the residual, the nonprofit specific residual interest **would not be paid until those target redemption amounts**." _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~15:16 PT)_

> Q: "The target redemption amount for Microsoft's $10,000,000,000 investment is **$60,000,000,000**. Is that a six times return on investment?" — A: " **Correct.**" _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~15:11 PT)_

> Q: "Did the board do any **quantitative analysis** to determine whether the six x return was appropriate?" — A: " **I don't know.**"
>
> Q: "Did the board do any **financial modeling** …?" — A: " **I don't know about specific financial modeling.**" _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~15:13 PT)_

> Wu: "\[The 2023 JDCA\] **does appear that OpenAI Inc., OpCo, licensed them all IP and technology as defined in this agreement excluding only AGI**." _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~15:17 PT)_

> Wu, on the **Watershed MOU**: " **Microsoft would receive — once there is a determination that there is AGI through this third-party panel — certain access to certain product IP.**" _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~15:20 PT)_

> Wu, on the September 2024 plan: "One of the considerations was **converting the LLC waterfall into a more traditional corporate stock ownership capital structure**. … **Stock that investors, employees, or others hold, would not be subject to a particular cap on profit distributions.**" _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~15:15 PT)_

> Q: "Does OpenAI Inc. have any full-time employees?" — A: " **I don't believe it has any full time employees** \[other than\] the board of directors, and Sam is the CEO." _(5/5/2026 Testimony @ ~15:09 PT)_
