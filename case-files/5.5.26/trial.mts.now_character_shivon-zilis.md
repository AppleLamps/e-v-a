**Shivon Zilis** is the **director of operations and special projects** at **[Neuralink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuralink)**. A former Bloomberg Beta investor and previously OpenAI **board observer**, she is one of the few witnesses with personal lines into both **[Musk](https://trial.mts.now/character/elon-musk)** and the OpenAI founders.

Plaintiffs' counsel **[Mr. Kry](https://trial.mts.now/character/robert-kry)** told the court at the end of **Day 4** that Zilis is on plaintiffs' live witness list and could take the stand **as soon as Tuesday May 5 (Day 6)**.
