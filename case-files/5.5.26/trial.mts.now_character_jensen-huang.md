**Jen-Hsun "Jensen" Huang** is the founder, president and chief executive officer of **[NVIDIA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NVIDIA)**. He appears in _Musk v. Altman_ through **photographic exhibits Ex. 70** and **Ex. 388**, which document Huang **personally delivering the first DGX-1 deep-learning supercomputer** to **[OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI)** in **August 2016** at **[Elon Musk](https://trial.mts.now/character/elon-musk)**'s request — a moment plaintiffs use to underscore Musk's hands-on role during OpenAI's founding period.
