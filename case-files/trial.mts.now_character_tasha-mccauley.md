**Tasha McCauley** is a former member of the **[OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI)** board of directors. A management scientist at the **RAND Corporation** and previously CEO of robotics company GeoSim Systems, McCauley joined the OpenAI board in 2018. She was one of the four directors — alongside **[Helen Toner](https://trial.mts.now/character/helen-toner)**, **[Ilya Sutskever](https://trial.mts.now/character/ilya-sutskever)** and **[Adam D'Angelo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_D'Angelo)** — who voted to **fire [Sam Altman](https://trial.mts.now/character/sam-altman)** as CEO on **November 17, 2023**, a decision reversed five days later.

Plaintiffs are expected to play her **video deposition** during their case.
