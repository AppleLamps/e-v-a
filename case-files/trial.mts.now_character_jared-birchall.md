**Jared Birchall** is the head of **Excession LLC**, [Elon Musk](https://trial.mts.now/character/elon-musk)'s family office, and **chief financial officer of the Musk Foundation**. Until shortly before trial he was also the **corporate secretary of [xAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/xAI)**.

He was plaintiffs' **second witness** in their case-in-chief, taking the stand on **Day 3 (April 30, 2026)** for direct examination by **[Mr. Kry](https://trial.mts.now/character/robert-kry)** and cross by Wachtell's **[Mr. Wilson](https://trial.mts.now/character/bradley-wilson)** and Dechert's **[Ms. Patel Gupta](https://trial.mts.now/character/nisha-patel-gupta)**. He was held subject to recall.

## Day 4: Bid testimony struck

On **Day 4 (May 1, 2026)**, the court granted defendants' motion to **strike** Birchall's testimony about Musk's **$97.4 billion February 2025 bid** for OpenAI's nonprofit assets, finding "blatant attempts to evade" prior orders. The judge specifically traced the offending question back to plaintiffs' co-counsel **[Marc Toberoff](https://trial.mts.now/character/marc-toberoff)**:

> "You shouldn't throw the young lawyers under the bus by not saying anything." `(050126TT.txt:360)`

The court instructed the jury that Birchall's bid testimony was to be disregarded.
