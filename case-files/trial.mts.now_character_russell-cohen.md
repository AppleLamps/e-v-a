**Russell P. Cohen** is a partner at **Dechert LLP** and the **lead trial counsel for [Microsoft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft)** in _Musk v. Altman_. He delivered **Microsoft's opening statement** on Day 1 and cross-examined **[Elon Musk](https://trial.mts.now/character/elon-musk)** briefly on his **2020 "captured by Microsoft" tweet**.

His Dechert trial team includes **[Ms. Patel Gupta](https://trial.mts.now/character/nisha-patel-gupta)** (who crossed [Birchall](https://trial.mts.now/character/jared-birchall)), **Howard Mark Ullman**, **Hannah Leone**, **John A. Jurata**, **Yosef Weitzman**, and **Forrest E. Lovett**.
