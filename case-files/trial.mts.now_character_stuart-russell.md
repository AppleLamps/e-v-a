**Stuart Jonathan Russell** is a British computer scientist, professor at the **University of California, Berkeley**, and co-author of _Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach_, the leading AI textbook. A long-standing voice in the **AI safety** debate, Russell founded UC Berkeley's **Center for Human-Compatible AI**.

Plaintiffs' counsel **[Mr. Kry](https://trial.mts.now/character/robert-kry)** told the court at the end of **Day 4** that Russell will be the **first live witness on Day 5 (Monday May 4, 2026)**, testifying as plaintiffs' AI safety expert. `(050126TT.txt:1985)`

## Expected testimony

Plaintiffs are expected to use Russell to:

- explain why an AGI lab's **governance structure** matters to safety;
- contrast a research nonprofit with a Microsoft-aligned for-profit; and
- address the distinct risks plaintiffs say a "captured" OpenAI poses.
