**Mira Murati** served as **chief technology officer** of **[OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI)** through the launch of [ChatGPT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT) and [GPT-4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-4), briefly held the title of interim CEO during the November 2023 board crisis, and departed in 2024 to found her own AI startup, **Thinking Machines Lab**.

Plaintiffs' counsel **[Mr. Kry](https://trial.mts.now/character/robert-kry)** told the court at the end of Day 4 that **Murati's video deposition will be played on the afternoon of Day 6 (Tuesday May 5)**, alongside designations from a Microsoft 30(b)(6) deponent and various corporate-rep clips. `(050126TT.txt:2010)`
