 1                                         [PROPOSED] ORDER

 2          Before the Court is Non-Party Ilya Sutskever’s Motion to Stay Magistrate Judge’s Order

 3 Pending Resolution of Motion for Relief From Nondispositive Pretrial Order (“Motion to Stay”)

 4 (Dkt. 337).

 5          Upon review and consideration of the Motion to Stay, the accompanying declarations and

 6 exhibits thereto, Plaintiffs’ Response in Opposition, and the accompanying declarations and exhibits

 7 thereto, and with good cause appearing therefor, IT IS HEREBY ORDERED THAT:

 8          The Motion to Stay is DENIED;

 9          Non-Party Ilya Sutskever SHALL immediately produce the “Brockman Memo” document

10 to Plaintiffs, free and clear of any obligation, restriction, or conditions on its use other than those

11 present in the protective orders entered in this case, if appropriate; and

12          Non-Party Ilya Sutskever SHALL sit for a second deposition concerning the value of his

13 financial interest in OpenAI and the Brockman Memo no later than November 5, 2025, or such date

14 as the parties and Ilya Sutskever may agree.

15

16          IT IS SO ORDERED.
