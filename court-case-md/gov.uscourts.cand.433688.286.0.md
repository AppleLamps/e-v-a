 1                                STIPULATION OF THE PARTIES

 2          WHEREAS, on December 12, 2024, Phase One Non-Party Reid Hoffman executed the

 3   Declaration of Reid Hoffman in Support of Microsoft Corporation (“Microsoft”), Reid Hoffman,
 4   and Deannah Templeton’s Opposition to Plaintiffs’ Motion for a Preliminary Injunction

 5   (“December 12 Declaration”) [Dkt. 65-21];

 6          WHEREAS, Microsoft has notified Plaintiffs and the OpenAI Defendants (collectively,

 7   the “Parties”) that the December 12 Declaration contains inadvertent errors that have recently
 8   come to light during discovery;

 9          WHEREAS, Microsoft and Mr. Hoffman wish to correct the December 12 Declaration

10   with a Corrected Declaration (attached as Exhibit A to Microsoft and Mr. Hoffman’s Stipulated
11   Motion for Administrative Relief) to ensure an accurate record;

12          NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree that Microsoft and Mr. Hoffman may file the

13   Corrected Declaration.

14          IT IS SO STIPULATED.
