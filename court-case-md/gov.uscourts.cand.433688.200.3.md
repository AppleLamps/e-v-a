 5    non-party Musk Foundation (“Responding Party”) responds and objects to the April 9, 2025

 7    Civil Action (“Subpoena”) issued by the OpenAI Defendants.

12    materiality, or admissibility of any documents or information, nor an agreement to submit to the

13    jurisdiction of the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California for purposes of

14    resolving disputes regarding this Subpoena.
