                1             I, ROBERT WU, declare as follows:
                2             1.    I serve as Deputy General Counsel for OpenAI OpCo, LLC, one of the defendants
                3    in this action. OpenAI OpCo, LLC is an entity within the broader OpenAI corporate family
                4    (“OpenAI”) through which OpenAI conducts business and raises capital. I focus on corporate
                5    transactional matters at OpenAI, including issues related to OpenAI’s capital raising.
                6             2.    I submit this declaration in connection with Defendants’ opposition to the
                7    preliminary injunction motion filed by Plaintiffs Elon Musk, Shivon Zilis, and X.AI Corp. (“xAI”)
                8    in the above captioned matter. I have personal knowledge of the facts set forth herein unless stated
                9    otherwise.
               10             3.    In October 2024, OpenAI closed a funding round in which it raised approximately
               11    $6.6 billion. I was the in-house attorney principally responsible for advising the company in
               12    connection with that funding round, and I have personally reviewed all of the agreements entered
               13    into with the investors who participated.
               14             4.    I understand that Plaintiffs have alleged, based on certain media reports, that
               15    OpenAI entered into agreements barring investors in the October 2024 funding round from
               16    investing in OpenAI’s competitors.
               17             5.    That allegation is false, and the reporting Plaintiffs rely on is inaccurate. OpenAI
               18    did not enter into any agreements, including with Microsoft, preventing investors from funding
               19    OpenAI competitors. Rather, a minority of the investors participating in the funding round were
               20    granted access to certain OpenAI confidential information on an ongoing basis, and those investors
               21    agreed to customary provisions governing their access to that information. In general, these
               22    investors would receive periodic access to OpenAI’s nonpublic financial statements and other
               23    nonpublic OpenAI information that is both commercially and competitively sensitive.
               24             6.    To protect against the potential disclosure or misuse of this nonpublic information,
               25    the agreements with these investors generally give OpenAI the right to terminate continuing access
               26    to the information should the investor obtain significant voting or other governance rights in an
               27    OpenAI competitor by virtue of an investment in that competitor. “Passive” investments in
               28    OpenAI’s competitors would not result in any change to the investors’ information rights. In

                1    general, the agreements provide that an investment is “passive” if the investor owns less than a

                2    certain threshold percentage of the voting power of a competitor’s securities and does not otherwise

                3    have specific rights with respect to the competitor’s corporate governance, e.g., the contractual

                4    right to appoint a member of the competitor’s board. The vast majority of investments would be

                5    deemed “passive” under these agreements, and therefore would not limit the investors’ access to

                6    OpenAI information.

                7             7.    None of these agreements restrict the ability of participants in the funding round to

                8    invest in OpenAI’s competitors—whether on a passive or a non-passive basis. In the event an

                9    investor subject to these provisions chose to pursue a non-passive investment in one of OpenAI’s

               10    competitors, the only consequence would be losing access to OpenAI’s nonpublic and

               11    commercially-sensitive information going forward.

               12             8.    In my experience, agreements of this type are customary in connection with

               13    investments in private technology companies, as they allow such companies to protect against

               14    potential misuse of their proprietary, and competitively-sensitive, information by competitors,

               15    thereby facilitating the investors’ meaningful and informed participation in the business. For

               16    example, the model “Investors’ Rights Agreement” maintained by the National Venture Capital

               17    Association—prepared by a national coalition of attorneys who specialize in venture capital

               18    financings—includes a term providing that a company has no obligation to provide a “Major

               19    Investor” with financial statements if that investor is a financial investment firm or collective

               20    investment vehicle that owns a threshold level of outstanding equity of any competitor and has a

               21    right to designate any members of the board of directors of a competitor. 1

               22             9.    Attached as Exhibit A is a true and correct copy of an executed letter agreement

               23    entered between OpenAI, Inc. and Microsoft Corporation, dated December 9, 2023, regarding

               24    “Board Observer Rights.”
