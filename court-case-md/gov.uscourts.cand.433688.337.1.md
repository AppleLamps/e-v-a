                    1          I, Simona Agnolucci, hereby declare as follows:
                    2          1.      I am a partner with the law firm Cooley LLP and I am counsel for Non-Party Ilya
                    3   Sutskever in the above-captioned action. I am duly licensed to practice law in the State of
                    4   California and am admitted to practice before this Court. The matters set forth herein are of my
                    5   own personal knowledge, and if called as a witness to testify, I could and would testify to these
                    6   matters. I submit this declaration in support of Mr. Sutskever’s Motion To Stay Magistrate Judge’s
                    7   Order Pending Resolution Of Motion For Relief From Nondispositive Pretrial Order.
                    8          2.      On October 18, 2025, I notified Plaintiffs counsel of Mr. Sutskever’s intent to object
                    9   to ECF No. 324 (“the Order”). See Exhibit A.
                   10          3.      As described in paragraphs 4-6 below, in an effort to resolve the parties’ disputes
                   11   without the need for further Court action, I engaged in telephonic meet and confer discussions with
                   12   Plaintiffs’ counsel on October 20 and 23, as well as the discussion by email attached hereto as
                   13   Exhibit A.
                   14          4.      On October 20, I spoke by telephone with Plaintiffs’ counsel and offered to resolve
                   15   the parties’ discovery dispute as follows: (1) Mr. Sutskever would provide immediate access to the
                   16   Brockman Memo, subject to the same terms 1 the parties had agreed to for access to a similar
                   17   document, the Altman Memo 2 and (2) Mr. Sutskever would agree to sit for a Zoom deposition
                   18   limited to questions necessary to determine the value of his financial stake in OpenAI.
                   19          5.      Plaintiffs’ counsel responded to my proposal during an October 23 telephone call,
                   20   during which they counter-proposed that Mr. Sutskever produce the Brockman memo and sit for a
                   21   deposition by Zoom regarding both the value of his financial stake in OpenAI and the content of
                   22   the Brockman memo.
