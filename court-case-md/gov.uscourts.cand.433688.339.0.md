                                  13          The Court’s October 17, 2025 discovery order ordered non-party Ilya Sutskever to produce

                                  14   a document and sit for a second deposition. ECF No. 324. He has done neither, and Plaintiffs

                                  15   now seek an order to show cause why he should not be held in contempt. ECF No. 335. In his

                                  16   motion to stay the discovery order, Sutskever points out that the Court did not set a deadline for

                                  17   compliance. ECF No. 337. Accordingly, the Court DENIES Plaintiffs’ request for an OSC

                                  18   without prejudice and ORDERS Sutskever to comply with the October 17 discovery order no

                                  19   later than November 5, 2025, unless he obtains a stay of that order from the district judge by

                                  20   November 5. Please note that asking for a stay (which he has done) and obtaining one are

                                  21   different things.

                                  22          IT IS SO ORDERED.
