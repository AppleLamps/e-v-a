 2          I, Marc Toberoff, declare as follows:
 3          1.     I am a member of the bar of the State of California and a principal of the law firm
 4 Toberoff & Associates, P.C., counsel of record for Plaintiff Elon Musk (“Plaintiff”). I submit this
 5 Declaration in support of the concurrently-filed Joint Stipulation Regarding Briefing Schedule on
 6 OpenAI Defendants’ Motion to Dismiss Plaintiff’s Complaint and Request for Judicial Notice
 7 (“Stipulation”). Except where otherwise stated, I have personal knowledge of the facts set forth
 8 herein and, if called as a witness, could and would so testify.
 9          2.     On October 8, 2024, OpenAI Defendants filed their Motion to Dismiss Plaintiff’s
10 Complaint and Request for Judicial Notice in support thereof (Dkt. Nos. 25 and 26, respectively;

11 collectively, “Motion”).
12          3.     On October 8, 2024, Leonard Horowitz filed a Motion to Intervene in this case
13 (Dkt. No. 24), to which a response is due October 22, 2024. I have attempted to call and email Mr.
14 Horowitz to discuss his motion and related scheduling, but he has not responded to any of my
15 communications.
16          4.     I observe the Jewish Holidays Yom Kippur (October 11-12, 2024) and Sukkot
17 (October 17-23, 2024).
18          5.     The combined effect of these intervening Holidays, and unanticipated Motion to
19 Intervene impede my ability to work on Plaintiff’s response to OpenAI Defendants’ Motion to
20 Dismiss, which, pursuant to Civ. L.R. 7-3, is due October 22, 2024.
21          6.     In light of these conflicts and the Commentary to Civ. L.R. 7-2 (providing that
22 “[f]or complex motions, parties are encouraged to stipulate to or seek a Court order establishing a
23 longer notice period with correspondingly longer period for response or reply”), on October 9,
24 2024, my colleague, Jaymie Parkkinen, reached out to OpenAI Defendants’ counsel to discuss an
25 extension of time to respond to their Motion or to amend the Complaint.
26          7.     The parties have agreed, subject to Court approval, that Plaintiff shall have until
27 November 14, 2024 to respond to OpenAI Defendants’ Motion or to amend the Complaint under
28 Fed. R. Civ. P. 15(a)(1)(B), and OpenAI Defendants should have until December 9, 2024 to reply
