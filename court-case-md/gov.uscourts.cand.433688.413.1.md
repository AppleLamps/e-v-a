 1                                  STIPULATION OF THE PARTIES

 2           WHEREAS, Defendant Microsoft Corporation’s Motion in Limine No. 1 (“MIL No. 1”)

 3    asks the Court to exclude evidence or argument at trial concerning the personal, family law
 4    litigation history of Microsoft’s damages expert, Jonathan I. Arnold, Ph.D.;

 5           WHEREAS, Microsoft has notified counsel for the OpenAI Defendants and Plaintiff Elon

 6    Musk (collectively, “the Parties”) of its intent to file excerpts of Dr. Arnold’s December 22, 2025

 7    deposition transcript as Exhibit A to MIL No. 1;
 8           WHEREAS, Microsoft has proposed narrow redactions to Exhibit A based on sensitive

 9    personal information concerning a third party;

10           WHEREAS, Microsoft seeks to maintain the information redacted in Exhibit A under seal
11    for the reasons set forth in its Administrative Motion to Seal Pursuant to Local Rules 7-11 and 79-

12    5, which it files concurrently herewith;

13           NOW, THEREFORE, subject to the Court’s approval, the Parties agree that this

14    information may be maintained under seal for purposes of Microsoft’s MIL No. 1.
15

16           IT IS SO STIPULATED.
