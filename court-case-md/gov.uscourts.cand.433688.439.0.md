17 respective discovery designations. Dkts. 429-2, 429-3, 429-4, 429-5. The Parties also submitted as

18 part of that filing a Proposed Order Regarding the Disputed Discovery Designations. Dkt. 429-6.

19          Following a meet-and-confer process, the Parties have agreed to amend their Joint Statement

21 designations to reduce the number of disputes presented to the Court. Accordingly, the Parties

22 hereby submit an amended Joint Statement of Disputed Discovery Designations, replacing the

23 document previously filed at Dkt. 429-1; amended discovery designations for each party, replacing

24 those previously filed at Dkts. 429-2, 429-3, 429-4, and 429-5; and an amended Proposed Order

25 Regarding the Disputed Discovery Designations, replacing the document previously filed at Dkt.

26 429-6.
