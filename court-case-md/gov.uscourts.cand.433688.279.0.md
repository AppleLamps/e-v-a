 1                                     ADMINISTRATIVE MOTION

 2          Defendant Microsoft Corporation (“Microsoft”) and Phase One Non-Party Reid Hoffman

 3   respectfully submit this Stipulated Administrative Motion Under L.R. 7-11 requesting leave to file

 4   a Corrected Declaration of Reid Hoffman (“Corrected Declaration”), attached hereto as Exhibit A,

 5   to correct an earlier declaration filed at Dkt. 65-21.

 6          Mr. Hoffman executed a declaration on December 12, 2024 in support of Microsoft and

 7   then-Defendants Hoffman and Deannah Templeton’s Opposition to Plaintiffs’ Motion for

 8   Preliminary Injunction. See Dkt. 65-21 (“December 12 Declaration”). The Court denied Plaintiff’s

 9   Motion for Preliminary Injunction in an Order dated March 4, 2025, which does not reference the

10   December 12 Declaration. See Dkt. 121; see also Dkt. 163 (Court’s Order Granting in Part and

11   Denying in Part Defendants’ Motions to Dismiss First Amended Complaint, similarly without

12   reference to the December 12 Declaration). The Corrected Declaration fixes two inadvertent errors

13   in the December 12 Declaration that have recently come to light during discovery:

14          1. The Corrected Declaration updates the dates of Mr. Hoffman’s service on OpenAI’s

15   board of directors, correcting the December 12 Declaration, which mistakenly states March 2018

16   to March 2023. Discovery has confirmed the correct dates as March 2019 to February 2023.

17          2. The December 12 Declaration states that Mr. Hoffman “abstained from voting on any

18   proposal relating to Microsoft’s investment in OpenAI on either the OpenAI or Microsoft boards.”

19   Dkt. 65-21 at Para. 6. Discovery has revealed that, as an OpenAI director, Mr. Hoffman authorized

20   a group of directors (excluding himself) to finalize the terms of a March 5, 2021 amendment to the

21   Joint Development and Collaboration Agreement (“JDCA”) between OpenAI and Microsoft and

22   later signed a unanimous written consent approving certain agreements, including a 2023

23   amendment to the JDCA. The Corrected Declaration addresses these points.

24          The above corrections are noted in the Redline attached hereto as Exhibit B. Microsoft has

25   notified Plaintiff and the OpenAI Defendants of its intent to file the Corrected Declaration in place

26   of the December 12 Declaration to address these errors. All parties have stipulated that Microsoft

27   and Mr. Hoffman may file the Corrected Declaration.                   See Joint Stipulation, filed

28   herewith. Microsoft and Mr. Hoffman reserve all rights to move to strike the December
