10          “District courts have broad discretion to appoint amici curiae.” Levin Richmond Terminal

11 Corp. v. City of Richmond, 482 F. Supp. 3d 944, 951 n.1 (N.D. Cal. 2020) (Rogers, J.) (granting

12 proposed amici curiae’s motions) (citing Hoptowit v. Ray, 682 F.2d 1237, 1260 (9th Cir. 1982),

13 abrogated on other grounds by Sandin v. Conner, 515 U.S. 472 (1995). Amici curiae can be

14 particularly useful in cases where the outcome affects the public beyond the parties to the case. See

15 Funbus Sys., Inc. v. Cal. Pub. Utils. Comm’n, 801 F.2d 1120, 1125 (9th Cir. 1986) (describing the

16 “classic role” of amici as “assisting in a case of general public interest” (citations omitted). Amici in

17 this case have unique information that can aid the court beyond the help provided by the parties’

18 lawyers. California by and through Becerra v. United States Department of the Interior, 381

19 F.Supp.3d 1153 (2019).

20          Amici curiae are former employees at OpenAI who collectively worked at the organization

21 between 2018 and 2024. During their tenures, which span the organization’s formative years

22 through its more recent development, amici held various technical and leadership positions within

23 the organization, including Research Scientists, Members of Technical Staff, Policy Researchers,

24 Research Leads and Policy Leads. Through their direct involvement in OpenAI’s operations and

25 development, amici possess unique firsthand knowledge of the organization’s founding principles,

26 research methodologies, governance structures, and technological capabilities. Amici have a

27 significant interest in this litigation as it addresses fundamental questions about OpenAI’s mission

28 and organizational structure that they helped shape during their employment.
