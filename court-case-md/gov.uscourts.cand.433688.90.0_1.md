 1          PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that, pursuant to Civil Local Rule 7-11, Plaintiffs Elon Musk,

 2 Shivon Zilis, and X.AI Corp. (collectively, “Plaintiffs”) will, and hereby do, move the Court for
 3 an order permitting the filing of their response, submitted herewith, to Defendants’ Objections to

 4 Evidence Submitted with Plaintiffs’ Reply in Support of Their Motion for a Preliminary
 5 Injunction.1 Dkt. Nos. 83 and 84. Defendants’ latest filing represents nothing more than
 6 procedural gamesmanship that will prejudice Plaintiffs if they are not afforded an opportunity to
 7 respond.2
 8 I.       BACKGROUND

 9          On November 29, 2024, Plaintiffs filed their Motion for a Preliminary Injunction. Dkt. 46.

10 In support of their Motion, Plaintiffs filed a declaration with 23 supporting exhibits. Id. On
11 December 13, 2024, the OpenAI and Microsoft Defendants filed separate memoranda (Dkt. Nos.
12 64 and 65, respectively) in opposition to Plaintiffs’ Motion. OpenAI filed a 25-page Opposition
13 memorandum together with two declarations and 21 exhibits. Dkt. No. 64. Microsoft filed a 24-
14 page Opposition memorandum attaching 6 declarations and 15 exhibits. Dkt. No. 65. On
15 December 27, 2024, Plaintiffs filed a consolidated 24-page Reply memorandum attaching 5
16 declarations and 29 exhibits, Dkt. No. 73, all of which directly respond to arguments made in
17 Defendants’ Oppositions. On January 3, 2025, OpenAI filed a Local Rule 7-3(d)(1) Objection to
18 Plaintiffs’ Reply evidence, Dkt. No. 83, which Microsoft joined that same day (collectively,
19 “Objections”), Dkt. No. 84.

24 injunction as well as to rebuttal evidence, and inaccurately contends that Plaintiffs do not rely on

 4          Because Plaintiffs submitted the challenged declarations and exhibits to rebut claims

 5 Defendants raised in their Oppositions and respond to their misleading evidence, there is simply
 6 no basis to object to Plaintiffs’ rebuttal evidence in reply under Local Rule 7-3(d)(1). See, e.g.,
 7 Huawei Technologies, Co., Ltd. v. Samsung Elec. Co., Ltd. 340 F.Supp.3d 934, 996 (N.D. Cal.
 8 2018) (explaining rebuttal evidence is proper when it is submitted to “contradict, impeach or
 9 defuse the impact of the evidence offered by an adverse party.”). Given that Defendants’
10 Objections are improper, Plaintiffs request leave to file a response to address these issues.
