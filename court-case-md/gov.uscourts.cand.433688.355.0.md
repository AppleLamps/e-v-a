 1          Pursuant to Civil Local Rule 7-11, Plaintiff Elon Musk (“Plaintiff ”) respectfully submits

 2 this unopposed administrative motion for leave to file a corrected version of Exhibit 57 to Plaintiff ’s

 3 Opposition to the OpenAI Defendants’ Motion for Summary Judgment. Plaintiff respectfully

 4 requests that the corrected version of Exhibit 57, filed under seal contemporaneously with this

 5 Motion, replace the existing version that was previously filed at Dkt. 351-60.

 6          The previously filed version of Exhibit 57 inadvertently included extraneous excerpts that

 7 were not cited in Plaintiff ’s opposition memorandum and were not intended to be included in the

 8 exhibit, but which include certain third-party confidential information. Plaintiff seeks to replace the

 9 previously filed version of Exhibit 57 with a corrected version that omits those extraneous pages.

10          Like the previously filed version of Exhibit 57, the corrected version is being filed

11 provisionally under seal. In accordance with Paragraph 12(b)(i)(1) of the Court’s Standing Order,

12 the reasons for sealing will be addressed in a forthcoming omnibus sealing stipulation or motion to

13 be filed after the conclusion of briefing on the OpenAI Defendants’ Motion for Summary Judgment.

14 Plaintiff respectfully requests that the Court provisionally maintain the corrected version of

15 Exhibit 57 under seal pending its ruling on that omnibus sealing stipulation or motion.

16          For the foregoing reasons, Plaintiff respectfully requests the Court grant this Motion. The

17 OpenAI Defendants have advised that they do not oppose this Motion or the relief sought herein.
