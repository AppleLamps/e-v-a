               1              I, Reid Hoffman, state and declare as follows:

               2              1.      I submit this Declaration to correct an earlier statement I made in a declaration

               3     sworn on December 12, 2024 (“December 12 Declaration”), and submitted to the Court in

               4     opposition to Plaintiffs’ Motion for a Preliminary Injunction. See Dkt. No 65-21. I have

               5     personal knowledge of all the facts stated herein, and if called as a witness could competently

               6     testify to them under oath.

               7              2.      As I explained in my December 12 Declaration, I am an internet entrepreneur

               8     and venture capitalist. I am active in all facets of the consumer internet and software industries,

               9     and my focus spans product development, innovation, business strategy, and finance. I am a

             10      co-founder of LinkedIn, and since 2009 I have worked as an investor at Greylock Partners,

             11      where I focus on building products that can reach hundreds of millions of participants. I have

             12      been involved with some of the best-known seed-stage and growth companies over the past

             13      two decades, including PayPal, Facebook, Zynga, Last.fm, Flickr, Mozilla, and OpenAI.

             14               3.      As I explained in my December 12 Declaration, I currently serve as an

             15      independent director on the board of Microsoft Corporation (“Microsoft”). I joined the board

             16      of Microsoft in March 2017, following its acquisition of LinkedIn, and have served

             17      continuously since that time as an independent director. Aside from my duties as an

             18      independent director, I have no operational role or responsibilities relating to generative AI or

             19      any other business at Microsoft, nor have I in the past.

             20               4.      As I explained in my December 12 Declaration, I was involved with OpenAI,

             21      Inc. since its inception, and joined its board in March 2019. I am providing this declaration to

             22      correct the dates that I served on the OpenAI board as a result of discovery into Phase One of

             23      this litigation. That discovery has clarified that my December 12 Declaration mistakenly stated

             24      that I joined OpenAI’s board in March 2018. As originally stated, throughout the time I was

             25      on the OpenAI board it was designated as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt nonprofit organization.

             26               5.      I resigned from OpenAI’s board in February 2023. This was shortly after

             27      OpenAI’s release of ChatGPT in November 2022 and around the time of its first paid version
