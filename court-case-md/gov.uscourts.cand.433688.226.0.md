                                  13          The OpenAI Defendants challenge 8VC’s claw back of 8VC000001 to 8VC000034. The

                                  14   Court has reviewed the documents in camera and now SUSTAINS 8VC’s invocation of the

                                  15   attorney-client privilege.

                                  16          The primary substantiation of the existence of an attorney-client relationship between Marc

                                  17   Toberoff and 8VC is the declaration of 8VC’s general counsel, Ian Shannon (ECF No. 221-2).

                                  18   Paragraphs 4 and 5 are sufficient to establish that there was an attorney-client relationship. This

                                  19   declaration is supported by paragraphs 3-5 of Toberoff’s own declaration (ECF No. 221-1), even if

                                  20   these paragraphs on their own fall short of establishing that the investors actually hired him.

                                  21   Shannon’s declaration establishes that fact in paragraph 4. Shannon’s declaration is additionally

                                  22   persuasive because he is the general counsel of 8VC, and it is normal for in-house counsel to be

                                  23   the ones to retain outside counsel.

                                  24          As for the documents themselves, without the declarations, they might be hard to

                                  25   understand. But with the declarations, it becomes clear that the legal advice Toberoff provided

                                  26   was almost entirely oral, and considering the subject matter, this is unsurprising. The documents

                                  27   are all communications surrounding the letter of intent that was ultimately sent to OpenAI. They

                                  28   are communications about having privileged phone calls and follow up after privileged phone calls
