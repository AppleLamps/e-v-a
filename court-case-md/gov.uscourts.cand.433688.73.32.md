 1                                          DECLARATION

 2          I, Jared Birchall, declare as follows:

 3          1.      I am the Chief Financial Officer of X.AI Corp. (“xAI”). I submit this declaration

 4 in support of Plaintiffs’ Motion for a Preliminary Injunction. I have personal knowledge of the

 5 facts stated in this declaration, except as otherwise noted, and if called as a witness, I could and

 6 would competently testify to them.

 7          2.      In October 2024, OpenAI conducted a funding round at a raised valuation of

 8 $157 billion which I understand involved a small group of prominent investors.

 9          3.      On or about November 1, 2024, I was informed by a renowned institutional

10 investor that, during the October 2024 funding round, Sam Altman expressly communicated to

11 them that their receipt of a capital allocation in the round was expressly conditioned on their

12 agreement to refrain from investing in OpenAI competitors.

13          4.      I was involved in coordinating xAI’s most recent funding round, which closed

14 December 5, 2024, and in that capacity, I interacted with potential investors in the round. During

15 discussions with potential investors, it became evident that they were well aware of OpenAI’s

16 directive discouraging investments in competing AI companies like xAI.

17          5.      For instance, on or about October 29, 2024, a reputable venture capital investor

18 and an investor in OpenAI, informed me that they would not invest in xAI because of Altman’s

19 funding restriction.

20          6.      I am not able to disclose the identities of the institutional investor and venture

21 capital investor at this time, because they provided this information to me on the condition that

22 I maintain their confidentiality.

23          7.      I declare under penalty of perjury of the laws of the United States that the

24 foregoing is true and correct. Executed on December 24, 2024.
