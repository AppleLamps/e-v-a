                                  14          The Court has reviewed Musk’s notice regarding fraud claims (Dkt. No. 485) and
                                  15   defendants’ responses (Dkt. Nos. 491, 492). In his notice, Musk stated that “so long as the Court
                                  16   plans to proceed to trial on the breach of charitable trust and unjust enrichment claims,” he
                                  17   “confirms that this notice may be treated as a notice of voluntary dismissal of [his fraud and
                                  18   constructive fraud] claims.” (Dkt. No. 485 at 2.) The Court plans to proceed to trial, including on
                                  19   Musk’s breach of charitable trust and unjust enrichment claims.
                                  20          Accordingly, the Court hereby DEEMS Musk’s fraud and constructive fraud claims—counts
                                  21   VII and VI of the second amended complaint—voluntarily DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE.
                                  22          IT IS SO ORDERED.
