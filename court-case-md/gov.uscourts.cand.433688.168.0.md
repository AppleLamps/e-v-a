17 “Counterclaim Defendants”) (“Counterclaims”);

18         WHEREAS, on April 29, 2025, the Parties filed a stipulation providing that Counterclaim

19 Defendants’ deadline to respond to the Counterclaims would be extended from April 30, 2025 to

20 May 7, 2025 (Dkt. 162);

21         WHEREAS, on May 1, 2025, the Court entered an order granting in part and denying in

22 part Defendants’ motions to dismiss Plaintiffs’ First Amended Complaint (Dkt. 163);

23         WHEREAS, the OpenAI Defendants’ amended answer to the First Amended Complaint is

24 currently due by May 15, 2025;

25         WHEREAS, Counterclaim Defendants filed a motion to dismiss the Counterclaims on

26 May 7, 2025, noticing a hearing on the motion for June 17, 2025 (the “Motion to Dismiss”) (Dkt.

27 166);

28         WHEREAS, Counterclaim Plaintiffs’ response to the Motion to Dismiss is currently due
