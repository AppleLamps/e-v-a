 1           On January 14, 2025, this Court heard oral argument on Plaintiffs Elon Musk, Shivon Zilis,

 2   and X.AI Corporation’s (“Plaintiffs”) Motion for a Preliminary Injunction against Samuel Altman,

 3   Gregory Brockman, OpenAI (including its various subsidiaries and corporate affiliates), Microsoft

 4   Corporation, Reid Hoffman, and Deannah Templeton (“Defendants”).

 5           It is Plaintiffs’ burden to establish (1) a likelihood of success on the merits, (2) that they are

 6   likely to suffer irreparable harm in the absence of an injunction, (3) that the balance of equities tips

 7   in their favor, and (4) that an injunction is in the public interest. Winter v. Nat. Res. Def. Council,

 8   Inc., 555 U.S. 7, 20 (2008). Plaintiffs fail to meet their burden.

 9           First, Plaintiffs are not likely to succeed on the merits of their antitrust claims that Microsoft
10   agreed not to fund OpenAI’s competitors (or agreed with others not to do so) in violation of 15

11   U.S.C. § 1 or that Microsoft, Hoffman, and Templeton participated in a board interlock in violation

12   of 15 U.S.C. § 19. Plaintiffs also lack antitrust injury, and Plaintiff Musk lacks antitrust standing.

13           Second, Plaintiffs have not shown that they are likely to suffer irreparable harm in the

14   absence of an injunction from a lack of investment capital, alleged board interlocks, or alleged

15   unspecified information exchanges.

16           Third, the balance of equities tips in Defendants’ favor, especially because enjoining

17   Microsoft’s use of OpenAI technology would harm not only Microsoft but also Microsoft’s

18   customers.

19           Fourth, the public interest favors denying the request for a preliminary injunction because
20   the costs to customers, developers, and users—all of whom depend on rapidly advancing AI

21   technology—from enjoining their use of OpenAI models would be a substantial and immediate

22   harm.

23           Accordingly, Plaintiffs’ motion for a preliminary injunction is DENIED.
