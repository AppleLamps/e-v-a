 7            1.     Never before has a corporation gone from tax-exempt charity to a $157 billion for-
 8    profit, market-paralyzing gorgon—and in just eight years. Never before has it happened, because

14    direction of Altman, Brockman, and Microsoft, fast becoming a fully for-profit subsidiary of
15    Microsoft. See infra ¶¶ 192-200.

22    generative artificial intelligence (“AI”) are now actively trying to eliminate competitors, such as

24    safety practices have devolved from shambolic at the time of the original Complaint to affirmatively
