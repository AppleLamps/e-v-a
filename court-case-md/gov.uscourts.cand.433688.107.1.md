                1             I, SAMUEL ALTMAN, declare as follows:
                2             1.    I serve as a director and as Chief Executive Officer of Defendant OpenAI, Inc. I
                3    have also been named as a defendant in the First Amended Complaint of Plaintiffs Elon Musk,
                4    Shivon Zilis, and X.AI Corp. (“xAI”).
                5             2.    I submit this declaration in connection with Defendants’ opposition to Plaintiffs’
                6    preliminary injunction motion in the above-captioned matter. I have personal knowledge of the
                7    facts set forth herein unless stated otherwise.
                8             3.    In October 2024, OpenAI closed a funding round in which it raised approximately
                9    $6.6 billion. A subset of the investors participating in that funding round were granted access to
               10    certain OpenAI confidential information on an ongoing basis, subject to customary provisions
               11    governing their access to that information.
               12             4.    I understand that Plaintiffs claim that I told investors in the October 2024 funding
               13    round that their ability to invest in OpenAI was contingent on forgoing investment in OpenAI’s
               14    competitors, including xAI.
               15             5.    That claim is false. I did not tell any investor in the October 2024 funding round that
               16    their ability to invest in OpenAI was subject to that condition, nor to my knowledge did anyone
               17    else at OpenAI.
               18             6.    Instead, certain investors who were to be granted access to OpenAI’s confidential
               19    information on an ongoing basis were informed that OpenAI would need to terminate their rights
               20    to that information if they made non-passive investments in OpenAI’s competitors.
               21             7.    That restriction is necessary to protect against the misuse of OpenAI’s
               22    competitively-sensitive information, and I understand it is industry standard for that reason.
               23             8.    In explaining these restrictions on investors’ information rights, I did not say that
               24    investors would lose the ability to invest in OpenAI if they chose to fund xAI or any other
               25    competitor.
