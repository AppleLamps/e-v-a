13   their monopoly, or near so, in generative artificial intelligence (“AI”) are now actively trying to

17   resigning in protest, or being forced out, and whole safety teams dissolved, all to make way for

18   “security” personnel whose real job is to facilitate military contracting. See infra ¶¶ 73, 191.

20   public are matters of degree, but the intent and effect of OpenAI’s actions to flout those

22   in good faith as a charity committed to safety and transparency above profit, organized for

23   public rather than private benefit, and working to avoid the undue concentration of powerful AI

24   technology. Defendants have admitted as much by their commitments to investors to convert

25   OpenAI to a fully for-profit enterprise.

14           203.    Not all AI is generative AI. For example, there are narrow AI systems trained
