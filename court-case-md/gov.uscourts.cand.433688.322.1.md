                    1      I, Anika Holland, hereby declare as follows:
                    2      1.          I am an associate with the law firm Cooley LLP and am counsel for Non-Party Ilya
                    3   Sutskever in the above-captioned action. I am duly licensed to practice law in the State of
                    4   California and am admitted to practice before this Court. The matters set forth herein are of my
                    5   own personal knowledge, and if called as a witness to testify, I could and would testify to these
                    6   matters. I submit this declaration in support of Mr. Sutskever’s Response to Plaintiffs’ Discovery
                    7   Letter Brief filed concurrently herewith.
                    8      2.          On April 28, 2025, Mr. Sutskever timely objected to Plaintiffs’ third-party document
                    9   subpoena under Rules 26, 34, and 45, and offered to meet and confer regarding the scope of any
                   10   document production.
                   11      3.          On May 13, 2025, and May 23, 2025, I, along with my colleague Eduardo
                   12   Santacana, met and conferred with Plaintiffs’ counsel, Jaymie Parkkinen of the law firm Toberoff
                   13   & Associates LLP. As a result of these conferences, the parties agreed on five categories of
                   14   responsive documents and communications, as well as a procedure for relevance redactions and
                   15   inspection. Specifically, Mr. Sutskever agreed to produce documents and communications related
                   16   to (1) the founding of OpenAI from 2015–2016, (2) decisions to open-source technology, (3) any
                   17   conflicts of interests of Sam Altman, Greg Brockman, and other members of the nonprofit board,
                   18   (4) safety, and (5) Sam Altman’s dishonesty.
                   19      4.          Counsel for Mr. Sutskever diligently reviewed documents for responsiveness
                   20   consistent with the parties’ agreement and produced nearly a thousand pages of documents. We
                   21   did not produce the Brockman Memo as it was not responsive to any of the five agreed-upon
                   22   categories.
                   23      5.          On September 5, 2025, I produced a tranche of responsive documents to Plaintiffs’
                   24   counsel and informed them it was Mr. Sutskever’s “second and final production.”
                   25      6.          Plaintiffs did not request the Brockman Memo ahead of Mr. Sutskever’s deposition.
                   26      7.          On October 14, 2025, I, along with my colleague Remy Carreiro, met and conferred
                   27   with Plaintiffs’ counsel to attempt to come to an agreement on Plaintiffs’ motions to compel further
                   28   discovery from Mr. Sutskever. Plaintiffs represented that they sought two things: (1) production
