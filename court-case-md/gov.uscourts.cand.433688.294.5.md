We are informed that the search terms used for the text extraction from Musk’s old phone backup
were as follows: “OpenAI”, “Open AI”, “OAI”, “Brockman”, “Altman”, “Ilya”, “gdb”, in each case from

originally proposed.

responsive texts between Elon Musk and Sam Teller. Both projects would require us to arrange for
additional data exports from the IT custodian, process that data for review, and then conduct a
review and analysis of the resulting documents. It is unreasonable to demand those additional steps
so late in the process when we are already heavily engaged in completing the linear reviews that
Judge Hixson ordered.
