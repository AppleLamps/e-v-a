 1          Pursuant to Civil Local Rule 7-11(b), Sam Altman, Greg Brockman, OpenAI, Inc., and

 2   affiliated OpenAI entities (the “OpenAI Defendants”) oppose Plaintiffs’ Administrative Motion for

 3   Leave to File a Response to the Amicus Brief of Kathleen Jennings in Her Capacity as the Attorney

 4   General of the State of Delaware (the “Amicus Brief”) (Dkt. No. 81).

 5          The Amicus Brief notifies the Court of the Attorney General’s “concurrent review” of

 6   OpenAI’s contemplated organization changes, and is not offered in support of, or in opposition to,

 7   any position taken by the parties. Dkt. No. 77-1 at 1. To the contrary, the Attorney General

 8   expressly “takes no position on the propriety of a preliminary injunction in this case.” Id. As such,

 9   there is no “[d]ue process” concern (Dkt No. 81 at 1) requiring a response from Plaintiffs. Instead,

10   as Plaintiffs’ proposed response makes clear, Plaintiffs seek leave to improperly rehash arguments

11   made in support of their preliminary injunction motion, while further assailing the OpenAI

12   Defendants. See Dkt. No. 81-1 at 3 (complaining of Defendants’ supposed “escalating self-

13   dealing,” “recent anticompetitive conduct,” and “extensive self-dealing”).

14          Denial of the requested leave is especially appropriate here because the request is

15   transparently tactical. Plaintiffs previously urged the Attorney General to become involved in the

16   matters here at issue. Attached to Plaintiffs’ proposed response is correspondence from November

17   14, 2024, transmitting Plaintiffs’ First Amended Complaint to the Delaware Department of Justice,

18   with an offer to provide further information. See Dkt. No. 81-3. Plaintiffs welcomed the Attorney

19   General’s engagement then, so long as it did not interfere with their preferred litigation strategy.

20   But now that the Attorney General has informed the Court that she is conducting the review

21   Plaintiffs claimed to want—but apparently doing so independently, not on Musk’s terms—

22   Plaintiffs propose a response denigrating her review as inadequate and demanding that the Court

23   ignore it. See Dkt. No. 81-1. Plaintiffs’ request should be denied.
