 1                                         DECLARATION

 2          I, Shivon Zilis, declare as follows:

 3          1.      I am a former member of OpenAI, Inc., among other concerns. I submit this

 4 declaration in support of Plaintiffs’ Motion for a Preliminary Injunction. I have personal

 5 knowledge of the facts stated in this declaration, except as otherwise noted, and if called as a

 6 witness, I could and would competently testify to them.

 7          2.      While I served as a member of OpenAI, Inc. and on its board, Sam Altman

 8 (“Altman”), OpenAI, Inc.’s CEO, caused ChatGPT to be publicly released on or about

 9 November 30, 2022, without informing the board prior to doing so.

10          3.      Shortly after ChatGPT was released, which the OpenAI board learned about

11 from Twitter, I raised concerns during a board meeting about making major product releases

12 without having sufficient process, governance, or oversight to ensure the safety of OpenAI’s

13 products.

14          4.      In 2021, I was informed that Altman invested in Helion Energy (“Helion”), a

15 nuclear energy startup.

16          5.      When a transaction between OpenAI and Helion was proposed, whereby

17 OpenAI would agree to purchase large quantities of electricity from Helion, I raised concerns

18 during a board meeting about the proposed contract. I was concerned about the speculative

19 nature of the investment in Helion’s technology, particularly given the absence of unconflicted
20 voices to speak to the validity of the technology.

21          6.      Based on the firmness of Altman’s intent to push forward despite these issues, I

22 became convinced that it would be futile to continue raising concerns with OpenAI, Inc.’s board

23 in the future.

24          7.      I declare under penalty of perjury of the laws of the United States that the

25 foregoing is true and correct. Executed on December _____, 2024.
