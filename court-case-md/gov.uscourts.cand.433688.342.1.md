                    2          1.      I am a partner with the law firm Cooley LLP and I am counsel for Non-Party Ilya

                   10          The Subpoena and Negotiated Scope (May - September 2025)
                   11          3.      Mr. Sutskever is a co-founder of OpenAI but is not a party to this action. In April
                   12   2025, Plaintiffs, then represented primarily by Toberoff & Associates, P.C. (“Toberoff”), served a
                   13   broad third-party subpoena on Mr. Sutskever. Recognizing the sensitivity of the subject matter, my
                   14   colleagues engaged in extensive, good-faith negotiations with Plaintiffs’ then-primary counsel
                   15   Jaymie Parkkinen to narrow the scope of the subpoena to relevant material.
                   16          4.      In May 2025, members of my team reached a binding agreement limiting Mr.
                   17   Sutskever’s production obligations to five specific categories: (a) the founding of OpenAI (2015–
                   18   2016); (b) decisions to open-source technology; (c) conflicts of interest of Sam Altman, Greg
                   19   Brockman, and other non-profit board members; (d) safety; and (e) Sam Altman’s dishonesty about
                   20   safety or the nonprofit structure (i.e., not including interpersonal or personnel issues). A true and
                   21   correct copy of an email memorializing that agreement is attached hereto as Exhibit A.
                   22          5.      We specifically invited Plaintiffs’ counsel to “[p]lease let us know if there were
                   23   additional categories they would like Mr. Sutskever to search for.” Exhibit A at 3. Plaintiffs
                   24   clarified the scope of two of the existing categories but did not request any additional categories.
