 1           I, Michael Wetter, state and declare as follows:

 2           1.        I joined Microsoft Corporation (“Microsoft”) as an employee in March 2007.

 3           2.        My current role at Microsoft is Corporate Vice President of Corporate

 4    Development.

 5           3.        As Corporate Vice President of Corporate Development, I am responsible for

 6    engaging with external partners and stakeholders relating to acquisitions and investments,

 7    including negotiating agreements, accessing and assessing diligence materials, and reviewing

 8    financial plans and analysis.

 9           4.        As part of this litigation, I was designated as Microsoft’s 30(b)(6) witness and

10    prepared myself to provide non-privileged testimony about a number of topics, including the

11    Joint Development and Collaboration Agreements (“JDCAs”) between Microsoft and OpenAI

12    and Microsoft’s investments in OpenAI, Inc.’s for-profit subsidiary beginning in 2019 and the

13    due diligence conducted by Microsoft in connection with those agreements.

14           5.        I submit this Declaration in support of Microsoft’s Motion for Summary

15    Judgment. I have personal knowledge of all the facts stated herein, and if called as a witness

16    could and would competently testify to them under oath.

17           6.        I first became aware of Microsoft’s partnership with the nonprofit OpenAI, Inc.,

18    and its for-profit subsidiary (collectively, “OpenAI”) at the time of the first JDCA and 2019

19    Investment. This investment was in the for-profit subsidiary. As part of my Corporate

20    Development role, beginning in 2021 through the present, I was directly involved in the

21    negotiations concerning Microsoft’s subsequent investments in OpenAI’s for-profit subsidiary

22    and was kept informed by others of the related JDCA amendments. In my role, I have become

23    familiar with the terms of the various agreements that relate to the Microsoft-OpenAI

24    collaboration.

25           7.        In connection with my preparation to testify as a 30(b)(6) witness in this case,

26    I learned about due diligence that Microsoft conducted prior to entering agreements with

27    OpenAI, principally before closing the 2019 Investment. As part of the diligence process,

28    Microsoft’s outside counsel reviewed materials provided by OpenAI, including documents
