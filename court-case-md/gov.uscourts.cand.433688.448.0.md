11          WHEREAS Count 4 of the Second Amended Complaint (Dkt. 170) for “Breach of Quasi-
12 Contract / Unjust Enrichment” (designated “Second Claim: Unjust Enrichment” in the pretrial

13 submissions and hereinafter referred to as the “Unjust Enrichment Claim”) named as defendants:

14 (1) Samuel Altman; (2) Gregory Brockman; (3) OpenAI, Inc.; (4) OpenAI, L.P.; OpenAI, L.L.C.;

15 OpenAI GP, L.L.C.; OpenAI OpCo, LLC; OpenAI Global, LLC; OAI Corporation, LLC; OpenAI

16 Holdings, LLC; Aestas Management Company, LLC; and Aestas, LLC (the “OpenAI For-Profit

17 Entities”); (5) OpenAI Startup Fund Management, LLC; OpenAI Startup Fund GP I, L.L.C.;

18 OpenAI Startup Fund I, L.P.; OpenAI Startup Fund SPV GP I, L.L.C.; OpenAI Startup Fund SPV

19 GP II, L.L.C.; OpenAI Startup Fund SPV GP III, L.L.C.; OpenAI Startup Fund SPV GP IV, L.L.C.;

20 OpenAI Startup Fund SPV I, L.P.; OpenAI Startup Fund SPV II, L.P.; OpenAI Startup Fund SPV

21 III, L.P.; and OpenAI Startup Fund SPV IV, L.P. (the “OpenAI Startup Fund Entities”); and

22 (6) Microsoft Corporation;

23          WHEREAS, on or about October 28, 2025, OpenAI, Inc., was renamed The OpenAI
24 Foundation;

25          WHEREAS, on or about October 28, 2025, OpenAI Group PBC was formed as a public
26 benefit corporation to undertake certain for-profit activities formerly carried out by some of the

27 OpenAI For-Profit Entities;

 1          WHEREAS, on January 15, 2026, the Court granted summary judgment in favor of

 2 Microsoft Corporation on the Unjust Enrichment Claim;

 3          WHEREAS, to streamline the issues for trial, Plaintiff seeks to revise the defendants against

 4 whom the Unjust Enrichment Claim is asserted;

 5          WHEREAS, the OpenAI Defendants’ counsel represent that they have authority to enter into

 6 this Stipulation on behalf of OpenAI Group PBC; and

 7          WHEREAS, the OpenAI Defendants deny that Plaintiff has shown any legal basis for

 8 asserting the Unjust Enrichment Claim against the OpenAI For-Profit Entities or the OpenAI Startup

 9 Fund Entities;

10          NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties stipulate as follows:

11          1.      OpenAI Group PBC shall be substituted for the OpenAI For-Profit Entities for

12 purposes of the Unjust Enrichment Claim, so that if liability is established against any of the OpenAI

13 For-Profit Entities on that claim then OpenAI Group PBC shall bear that liability;

14          2.      The Unjust Enrichment Claim is hereby dismissed with prejudice as against the

15 OpenAI For-Profit Entities, with the parties bearing their own costs, and OpenAI Group PBC is

16 added as a defendant named in that claim;

17          3.      The Unjust Enrichment Claim is hereby dismissed with prejudice as against the

18 OpenAI Startup Fund Entities, with the parties bearing their own costs;

19          4.      This Stipulation shall have no effect on the Unjust Enrichment Claim against Samuel

20 Altman, Gregory Brockman, or OpenAI, Inc. (now known as The OpenAI Foundation) or on the

21 relief that might otherwise be ordered against any of those defendants; and

22          5.      The Stipulation shall have no effect on the Parties’ rights with respect to the

23 dismissed Unjust Enrichment Claim against Microsoft Corporation, including any rights on appeal.
