 1          The OpenAI Defendants oppose Plaintiffs’ motion to extend the deadlines for expert
 2   discovery by another five weeks. The schedule for completing discovery in this expedited action
 3   has already been extended once at Plaintiffs’ request. Plaintiffs have not shown good cause for the
 4   further extension they now seek, which would complicate the deposition program for experts, upset
 5   the schedule of witnesses who have arranged to make themselves available for deposition in the
 6   period contemplated by the ordered timetable, and compress the parties’ time to meet the Court’s
 7   Pretrial Compliance Deadline and prepare for trial.
 8          The OpenAI Defendants believe the current schedule ordered by the Court on July 25, 2025
 9   is appropriate and that the parties should proceed according to those deadlines. The OpenAI
10   Defendants do not, however, oppose a shorter extension of one or two weeks, which would provide
11   a month between the end of fact depositions and the submission of opening expert reports, while
12   enabling expert depositions to be completed before the Christmas holiday period.
13   I.     THERE IS NO GOOD CAUSE FOR PLAINTIFFS’ PROPOSED EXTENSION OF
            EXPERT DISCOVERY.
14

15          Plaintiffs have not shown good cause for their proposed extension of the expert discovery
16   deadlines. The OpenAI Defendants produced of the bulk of OpenAI’s financial information called
17   for by Plaintiffs’ requests for production—including valuation materials, audited financials, and
18   investor presentations—by the August 4, 2025 deadline for the Substantial Completion of
19   Document Discovery. Plaintiffs and their experts have had access to that information for more than
20   a month and a half, and will have had it for more than two and a half months before they are required
21   to submit opening expert reports.
22          Plaintiffs complain about the OpenAI Defendants’ recent reproduction of approximately
23   137 documents on September 13, 2025 promptly following a discovery ruling by Magistrate Judge
24   Hixson. Dkt. No. 251. Plaintiffs will have had access to these documents in unredacted form for
25   more than a month before opening expert reports are due. And most of those documents—which
26   represent a tiny fraction of the documents the OpenAI Defendants have produced in this action—
27   were produced to Plaintiffs with only targeted redactions weeks earlier, on August 25.
28          Plaintiffs also point to a fourteen-page Memorandum of Understanding entered by OpenAI

16   Defendants’ overall production in this case—does not provide good cause to upend the expert

17   discovery schedule.

18   II.    PLAINTIFFS’ PROPOSED EXTENSION OF THE EXPERT DISCOVERY
            DEADLINES IS UNWORKABLE AND PREJUDICES PREPARATION FOR
19          TRIAL.

21
     have relied on the existing deadlines in case planning and to ensure expert availability during the
22
     current deposition window. Plaintiffs now propose that rebuttal expert reports be exchanged on
23
     December 15, which would require most of the expert depositions to occur during the Christmas
24
     and New Year holiday period, when availability would be understandably limited. This schedule
25
     is not practical and will invariably necessitate further extensions well into January 2026 to complete
26
     expert depositions.

            There is no reason that discovery in this expedited action cannot be completed this year.
