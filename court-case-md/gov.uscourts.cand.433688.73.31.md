 1                                         DECLARATION

 2          I, Gavin Baker, declare as follows:

 3          1.     I am the Managing Partner and Chief Investment Officer at Atreides

 4 Management, LP, a crossover investment firm which manages approximately $4 billion in

 5 capital assets. I submit this declaration in support of Plaintiffs’ Motion for a Preliminary

 6 Injunction. I have personal knowledge of the facts stated in this declaration, except as otherwise

 7 noted, and if called as a witness, I could and would competently testify to them.

 8          2.     As widely reported, in October 2024, OpenAI conducted a funding round that

 9 elevated its valuation to $157 billion and set the minimum investment allocation at $250 million.

10 The round was oversubscribed. In my experience, high-value tech investors that can invest at

11 such levels comprise a small selective group, who know each other professionally and

12 frequently invest in the same offerings.

13          3.     It was common knowledge and widely understood in the investment community

14 I work in that during the October 2024 funding round, OpenAI told investors that an allocation

15 in its October 2024 offering was contingent on their commitment to not fund AI competitors,

16 specifically calling out xAI, Anthropic and others.

17          4.     I am aware that Mr. Altman, in his December 4, 2024 interview at the 2024 New

18 York Times Dealbook Summit, suggested that the competition restriction placed on investors in

19 OpenAI’s October 2024 round was limited to “information rights.” This directly contradicts the

20 consistent understanding and belief within our investment community as to what OpenAI told
21 investors and what investors clearly understood OpenAI’s unequivocal investment condition to

22 be, i.e., that those who invest in xAI and other OpenAI competitors, would be denied an

23 investment allocation in OpenAI’s October 2024 and future funding rounds.

24          5.     I declare under penalty of perjury of the laws of the United States that the
