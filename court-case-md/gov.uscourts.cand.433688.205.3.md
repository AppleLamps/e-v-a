 1            46.   The change the board ultimately approved was the creation of a “capped”

 2   for-profit entity, OpenAI, L.P. The new for-profit was bound to pursue the nonprofit’s mission

 3   and subject to the control of the OpenAI, Inc. board, but presented investors and employees with

 4   the opportunity to participate in any profits OpenAI’s operations might ultimately yield. These

 5   participation interests were “capped”—their holders could see returns up to a certain fixed point,

 6   with any residual profits flowing to the nonprofit. This capped-profit structure remains in place

 7   today.

 9   OpenAI announced it in a blog post in early March 2019. Musk had advance notice; he was

10   offered, and declined, equity in the new entity. The day the blog post went live, Musk asked

11   Altman to make clear to others that he had “no financial interest in the for-profit arm of OpenAI.”

12            48.   Musk raised no objection to the formation of OpenAI, L.P.
