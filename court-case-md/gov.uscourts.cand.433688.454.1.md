12 I.       MUSK’S POLITICAL ACTIVITIES SHOULD BE EXCLUDED

13          Musk has played a prominent role in American politics, both as a recent government official

14 and as an outspoken private citizen. His recent role in the White House, relationship with Donald

15 Trump, and personal political commentary are not relevant to his claims in this case. Defendants

16 do not seem to care. At Musk’s deposition, over staunch objection, OpenAI’s counsel repeatedly

17 asked Mr. Musk about his role in the White House and “frequent contact” with President Trump.

18 Ex. 1 (Musk Tr.) at 15:20-16:10, 17:23-25, 19:25-20:14. Defendants now intend to drag this

19 sideshow into the courtroom. For example, OpenAI has designated a trial exhibit discussing Musk’s

20 leadership of the White House’s Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE). OpenAI Trial Ex.

21 1021 (Ex. 2). Testimony or evidence on these topics serves no function other than to provoke the

22 jury and should be excluded in its entirety.

23          Evidence of “political affiliation and past political activities generally is inadmissible.”

24 United States v. Kaneshiro, 2024 WL 118415, at *7-8 (D. Haw. Mar. 14, 2019). That is particularly

     III.   GRATUITOUS EVIDENCE OF MUSK’S FAMILY LIFE SHOULD BE
 6          EXCLUDED

 7          Finally, the Court should tightly limit evidence of Musk’s family life at trial to the bounds

 8 of relevance. Shivon Zilis worked for Musk. She served as an advisor to OpenAI and later joined

 9 OpenAI’s board in 2018 after Musk stepped down. It is publicly known that, while serving on

10 OpenAI’s board, Zilis became pregnant by IVF with Musk. But that relationship did not impact

11 Zilis’s board duties. Indeed, when she disclosed this fact to the OpenAI board, they found that no

12 conflict existed, and she remained on OpenAI’s board until Musk founded xAI.

13          Defendants nonetheless used this history to probe into Musk’s family life throughout

14 discovery. OpenAI asked Zilis, for example, about her current living arrangement with Musk and

15 their young children and elicited details about Musk’s role as a father. See, e.g., Ex. 4 (Zilis Tr.) at

16 11:6-12:4; 307:12-21. They have designated this completely improper testimony for use at trial as

17 well. That evidence should be excluded.

18          The Court should not permit Defendants to pry into details of Musk or Zilis’s homes, their

19 children, or similarly private details. Evidence of Musk’s other family relationships is also wholly

20 irrelevant to the case. It is well known that Musk has multiple other children with past partners.

21 Any testimony or evidence on this subject should be excluded as irrelevant and unduly prejudicial.
