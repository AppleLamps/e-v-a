 2          Pursuant to Civ. L.R. 6-1(b) and 6-2 and paragraph 3 of the Court’s Standing Order in
 3 Civil Cases, Plaintiff Elon Musk and Defendants Samuel Altman, Gregory Brockman, OpenAI,
 4 Inc., OpenAI L.P., OpenAI, L.L.C., OpenAI GP, L.L.C., OpenAI OpCo, LLC, OpenAI Global,
 5 LLC, OAI Corporation, LLC, OpenAI Holdings, LLC, OpenAI Startup Fund Management, LLC,
 6 OpenAI Startup Fund GP I, L.L.C., OpenAI Startup Fund I, L.P., OpenAI Startup Fund SPV GP I,
 7 L.L.C., OpenAI Startup Fund SPV GP II, L.L.C., OpenAI Startup Fund SPV GP III, L.L.C.,
 8 OpenAI Startup Fund SPV GP IV, L.L.C., OpenAI Startup Fund SPV I, L.P., OpenAI Startup
 9 Fund SPV II, L.P., OpenAI Startup Fund SPV III, L.P., OpenAI Startup Fund SPV IV, L.P.,
10 AESTAS Management Company, LLC, and AESTAS LLC (collectively, the “OpenAI

11 Defendants” and together with Plaintiff, the “Parties”), by and through their counsel, hereby
12 stipulate and request as follows:
13          WHEREAS, on October 8, 2024, OpenAI Defendants filed a Motion to Dismiss Plaintiff’s
14 Complaint and a Request for Judicial Notice in support thereof (Dkt. Nos. 25 and 26, respectively;
15 collectively, “Motion”), and set a hearing for November 12, 2024;
16          WHEREAS, pursuant to Civ. L.R. 7-3 Plaintiff must respond to OpenAI Defendants’
17 Motion by October 22, 2024 and OpenAI Defendants must reply to Plaintiff’s response by
18 October 29, 2024;
19          WHEREAS, Commentary to Civ. L.R. 7-2 provides: “For complex motions, parties are
20 encouraged to stipulate to or seek a Court order establishing a longer notice period with
21 correspondingly longer period for response or reply.”;
22          WHEREAS, on October 8, 2024, Leonard Horowitz filed a Motion to Intervene in this
23 case (Dkt. No. 24), to which the Parties must respond by October 22, 2024;
24          WHEREAS, in addition, the intervening Jewish Holidays (e.g., Yom Kippur (October 11-
25 12) and Sukkot (October 17-23)) impede Plaintiff’s counsel’s ability to simultaneously respond to
26 the OpenAI Defendants’ Motion and the Motion to Intervene;
27          WHEREAS, on October 11, 2024, the Court ordered the Parties to file a Joint Statement
28 outlining the elements of Plaintiff’s causes of action to be filed by October 16, 2024, vacated the

                                                     3
                   JOINT STIPULATION AND [PROPOSED] ORDER RE BRIEFING SCHEDULE
 1 November 12, 2024 hearing on the Motion, and continued the Case Management Conference to
 2 December 16, 2024 (Dkt. No. 27);
 3          WHEREAS, modifying the time for Plaintiff’s response and OpenAI Defendants’ reply in
 4 connection with the Motion as set forth below will not interfere with any dates set in this action;
 5 and
 6          WHEREAS, the Parties have conferred over a proposed modification to the briefing
 7 schedule on the Motion and agree good cause exists to modify the time to respond and reply to the
 8 Motion.
 9          IT IS HEREBY STIPULATED AND AGREED by the Parties that:
10             1. Plaintiff shall have until November 14, 2024 to respond to OpenAI Defendants’

11                 Motion by filing an Opposition or amending the Complaint under Fed. R. Civ. P.
12                 15(a)(1)(B);
13             2. OpenAI Defendants shall have until December 9, 2024 to reply to Plaintiff’s
14                 response to the Motion;
15             3. For the avoidance of doubt, all ADR and other deadlines attendant to the Case
16                 Management Conference are reset based on the new date of December 16, 2024;
17                 and
18             4. All other deadlines remain unchanged.
