 1          Pursuant to Civil Local Rule 7-11 and 79-5(f), the OpenAI Defendants hereby move to
 2   file under seal their Opposition to Plaintiff’s Motion in Limine No. 4 filed concurrently with this
 3   administrative motion.
 4          Plaintiff moved to file much of his Motion in Limine No. 4 under seal (Dkt. 405). The
 5   OpenAI Defendants do not believe that any of the information in Plaintiff’s Motion in Limine No.
 6   4 is sealable under the applicable standards. However, because the OpenAI Defendants’
 7   Opposition to Plaintiff’s Motion in Limine No. 4 and Exhibits B and C thereto reflect information
 8   that Plaintiff has moved to seal, the OpenAI Defendants are filing these documents under seal in
 9   an abundance of caution. Pursuant to Civ. L.R. 79-5(f)(3), Plaintiff bears the burden of
10   establishing that his materials are sealable, so he must “file a statement and/or declaration as
11   described in subsection (c)(1)” of Civil Local Rule 79-5 within seven days should he wish to keep
12   any portion of the Opposition under seal. Id.
13          Accordingly, the OpenAI Defendants have provisionally filed under seal the documents
14   listed below:
