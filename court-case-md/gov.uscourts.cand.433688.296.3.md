 1                                            DECLARATION

 2          I, Jaymie Parkkinen, declare as follows:

 3          1.     I am an attorney with Toberoff & Associates, P.C., counsel for Plaintiff in Musk, et

 4 al. v. Altman, et al., No. 4:24-cv-04722-YGR (N.D. Cal.). Except as otherwise noted, I have

 5 personal knowledge of the facts stated herein and, if called, could and would testify competently

 6 thereto. I submit this declaration in support of Plaintiff ’s Motion to Authorize Alternative Service

 7 of Deposition Subpoena and Compel Compliance (the “Motion”).

 8          2.     In August 2025, Plaintiff issued a document subpoena to Ermira Murati. I am

 9 informed that a registered process server attempted to effect service of the subpoena at

10                                          (“Residence One”) – which was believed to be Ms. Murati’s

11 residence – at least five times over the course of two weeks. A Proof of Service dated August 18,

12 2025, so stating, is attached as Exhibit 1.

13          3.     I am also informed that in August 2025, a registered process server attempted to

14 effect service of the same subpoena at                                                     (“Residence

15 Two”)—another residence believed to be Ms. Murati’s—at least four times over the course of two

16 weeks. A Proof of Service dated August 18, 2025, so stating, is attached as Exhibit 2.

17          4.     On September 11, 2025, Plaintiff issued a subpoena for Ms. Murati’s deposition.

18 That subpoena is attached as Exhibit 3. I am informed that a registered process server effected

19 service of that subpoena at Ms. Murati’s place of work, Thinking Machines Lab, 2300 Harrison

20 Street, San Francisco, CA 94110, by delivering the subpoena to                        , Front Desk

21 Security, who represented he was authorized to accept service for Ms. Murati. A Proof of Service

22 dated September 11, 2025, so stating, is attached as Exhibit 4.

23          5.     When Plaintiff was unable to make contact with Ms. Murati, Plaintiff issued another

24 deposition subpoena on September 15, 2025. That subpoena is attached as Exhibit 5. I am

25 informed that between September 15 and September 19, 2025, a registered process server made five

26 separate attempts to personally serve that subpoena on Ms. Murati at Thinking Machines Lab, 2300

27 Harrison Street, San Francisco, CA 94110. A Proof of Service dated September 22, 2025, so stating,

28 is attached as Exhibit 6.

 1          6.      In parallel, Plaintiff engaged investigators through the litigation services firm

 2 Nardello & Co. LLC to confirm Ms. Murati’s location and attempt to serve her.

 3          7.      On September 29, 2025, Plaintiff again issued a revised deposition subpoena for Ms.

 4 Murati. That subpoena is attached as Exhibit 7.

 5          8.      On September 29, 2025, Nardello & Co. LLC informed me that U.S. public records

 6 confirmed that Ms. Murati was in fact associated with the Residence Two address within the past

 7 year.

 8          9.      I am informed that on September 30, 2025, investigators engaged by Nardello & Co.

 9 LLC attempted to personally serve Ms. Murati’s deposition subpoena at Thinking Machines Lab

10 and at Residence Two. I am informed that neither attempt was successful, but a copy of the service

11 packet was left with a plain clothes security guard at Residence Two. Proofs of Service, so stating,

12 are attached as Exhibit 8. The Declaration of Michael Conner, filed in conjunction with the

13 Motion, further explains these events.

14          10.     I am informed that after the September 30, 2025 efforts were made at Residence Two

15 and Ms. Murati’s workplace, a copy of the deposition subpoena was sent to those addresses via

16 FedEx overnight delivery, and was confirmed delivered at both addresses on October 1, 2025. A

17 Proof of Service, so stating, is attached as Exhibit 9.

18          11.     I am informed that on October 1, 2025, an investigator from Nardello & Co. LLC

19 again attempted service at Thinking Machines Lab’s office and Residence Two, and that security

20 personnel at both locations again declined to accept service. Proofs of Service, so stating, are

21 attached as Exhibit 10.

22          12.     Neither a stipulation under Civil L.R. 7-12, nor a meet and confer under Civil L.R.

23 37-1, have been possible with respect to the relief requested in the Motion due to Plaintiff ’s

24 inability to make contact with Ms. Murati or her counsel, as described herein and in the Motion.

25          13.     I declare under penalty of perjury that the foregoing is true and correct.
