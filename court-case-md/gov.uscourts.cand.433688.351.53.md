Each year since 2012, the world has seen a new step function advance in Al
capabilities. Though these advances are across very different fields
like vision(opens in a new window) (2012), simple video games(opens in a new

approach: innovative applications of deep neural networks coupled with
increasing computational power. But still, Al system building today involves a lot
of manual engineering for each well-defined task.

In contrast, an AGI will be a system capable of mastering a field of study to the
world-expert level, and mastering more fields than any one human—like a tool
which combines the skills of Curie, Turing, and Bach. An AGI working on a
problem would be able to see connections across disciplines that no human
could. We want AGI to work with people to solve currently intractable multi-
disciplinary problems, including global challenges such as climate change,
affordable and high-quality healthcare, and personalized education. We think its
impact should be to give everyone economic freedom to pursue what they find
most fulfilling, creating new opportunities for all of our lives that are
unimaginable today.

OpenAl is producing a sequence of increasingly powerful Al technologies, which
requires a lot of capital for computational power. The most obvious way to cover
costs is to build a product, but that would mean changing our focus. Instead, we
intend to license some of our pre-AGI technologies, with Microsoft becoming our
preferred partner for commercializing them.

We believe that the creation of beneficial AGI will be the most important
technological development in human history, with the potential to shape the
trajectory of humanity. We have a hard technical path in front of us, requiring a
unified software engineering and Al research effort of massive computational
scale, but technical success alone is not enough. To accomplish our mission of
ensuring that AGI (whether built by us or not) benefits all of humanity, we'll need
to ensure that AGI is deployed safely and securely; that society is well-prepared
for its implications; and that its economic upside is widely shared. If we achieve
this mission, we will have actualized Microsoft and OpenAl's shared value of
empowering everyone.
