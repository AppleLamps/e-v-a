                                  15          Second, Sutskever’s argument that Magistrate Judge Hixson failed to weigh his
                                  16   “fundamental right to privacy in financial information” misconstrues the order. Magistrate Judge
                                  17   Hixson found that “Sutskever’s claimed right to privacy does not bar this discovery,” citing two

                                  21   other contrary to law. Moreover, Sutskever’s cited cases do not support the proposition for which
                                  22   they are cited. None of those cases discuss applying a “extra protection” for nonparties while also
                                  23   weighing relevance and “the seriousness of the privacy intrusion.” See e.g. High Tech Med.
                                  24   Instrumentation, Inc. v. New Image Indus., Inc., 161 F.R.D. 86 (N.D. Cal. 1995) (discusses
