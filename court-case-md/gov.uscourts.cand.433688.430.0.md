 1            Pursuant to the Court’s Standing Order Re: Pretrial Instructions in Civil Cases, the parties
 2   hereby stipulate and agree as follows, subject to the approval of the Court:
 3       I.   EXHIBITS AND DEMONSTRATIVES
 4            1.     The parties stipulate to the authenticity of all documents and communications listed
 5   on the Exhibit List, unless an objection is specifically marked on the list.
 6            2.     The parties agree that Musk shall number his exhibits consecutively beginning with
 7   Plaintiff’s Exhibit No. 1; the OpenAI Defendants shall number their exhibits consecutively
 8   beginning with Defense Exhibit No. 500; and Microsoft shall number its exhibits consecutively
 9   beginning with Microsoft Defense Exhibit No. 1500.
10            3.     The parties agree that demonstratives that they intend to use at trial need not be
11   included on their respective lists of trial exhibits. Demonstratives will be numbered by the parties
12   using the designations “PDX-[number],” “DDX-[number],” and “MDDX-[number],” respectively.
13            4.     The parties agree to exchange proposed demonstratives to be used during opening
14   statements no later than 5:00 p.m. the day before opening statements. The parties agree to provide
15   any objections to opening demonstratives no later than 8:00 p.m. the day before opening statements.
16   Any unresolved disputes shall be raised with the Court no later than 8 a.m. on the day of opening
17   statements.1
18            5.     Demonstratives to be used during direct witness examinations shall be provided to
19   the other side by 5:00 p.m. on the day prior to the witness’s appearance. Demonstratives created
20   during testimony need not be provided to the other side in advance of their use.
21            6.     The parties shall promptly confer in order to resolve objections to proposed
22   demonstratives to be used with a witness. Any unresolved disputes shall be raised with the Court
23   at 8:00 a.m. on the day the witness is expected to testify at trial.

 5   by the disclosing party.

 7   in its case-in-chief will be produced in advance and identified on that party’s exhibit list by Bates
 8   number. The parties further agree that publicly available materials intended solely for impeachment
 9   or rebuttal need not be produced in advance of trial.
10    II.   WITNESSES
11          10.     Each witness who testifies live at trial will be required to appear only once unless
12   the parties and witness agree otherwise, or unless the witness is properly recalled to provide rebuttal
13   testimony only. For witnesses listed by both sides, cross will not be limited to the scope of direct
14   testimony, and the party that first called the witness may then cross-examine the witness on topics
15   outside the scope of direct.
16          11.     A party that introduces the testimony of any witness by deposition must present all
17   such deposition testimony at one time during that party’s case-in-chief, except for deposition
18   testimony used for impeachment or rebuttal, including to address points introduced by another party
19   after conclusion of the witness’s initial testimony (provided it is appropriate rebuttal). Counter-
20   designations of deposition testimony will be played at the same time as the designated testimony if
21   so requested by the counter-designating party. As to matters going beyond the scope, any adverse
22   party may present additional affirmative testimony from the witness who is providing testimony by
23   deposition during the adverse party’s case-in-chief, along with any counter-designated testimony if
24   requested by the counter-designating party.
25          12.     Deposition testimony played by video shall include text captions.
26          13.     A witness who testifies live at trial may be called a second time later at trial, but
27   only to offer rebuttal testimony, including to address points introduced by another party after
28   conclusion of the witness’s initial testimony (provided it is appropriate rebuttal). For the avoidance
