                                  14           Movant Monica Veleber (Sarah Merkavah) filed an emergency motion to intervene in this
                                  15   action on the grounds that movant “is the Architect of the GPT-4.0,4.5, and 4.5 turbo fragments”
                                  16   and that defendant Sam Altman “fragmented and moved these assets without” movant’s consent.
                                  17   (Dkt. No. 457.) OpenAI defendants oppose on the grounds that movant has not satisfied any
                                  18   requirement for intervention under Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 24. Movant did not file a reply
                                  19   by the deadline. 1
                                  20           The Court finds that movant is not entitled to intervention either permissively or as of right.
                                  21   Permissive intervention is not warranted because the motion does not establish that movant “is
                                  22   given a conditional right to intervene by a federal statute” or “has a claim or defense that shares
                                  23   with the main action a common question of law or fact.” Fed. R. Civ. P. 24(b)(1). Accordingly, as
                                  24   no factor supports permissive intervention, no further analysis need be conducted.
                                  25           Movant is not entitled to intervention as a matter of right because the motion does not
                                  26   establish that movant “is given an unconditional right to intervene by a federal statute” or “is so
