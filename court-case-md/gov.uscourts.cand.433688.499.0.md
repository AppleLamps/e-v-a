 1             Pursuant to Section 3(e) of the Court’s Standing Order re: Pretrial Instructions in Civil Cases

 2 (last updated March 17, 2025), Plaintiff Elon Musk, the OpenAI Defendants, and Defendant

 3 Microsoft Corporation hereby submit this Amended Joint Exhibit List. This Amended Joint Exhibit

 4 List reflects the Parties’ efforts to narrow the issues in dispute by engaging in a continued meet and

 5 confer process following the filing of the Amended Joint Exhibit List on March 12, 2026.

 6             The Parties have assigned the following numeric designations: Musk (Exhibits 1-499);

 7 OpenAI (Exhibits 500-1499); Microsoft (Exhibits 1500-1601).

 8             Appendix A to this Amended Joint Exhibit List includes documents that the Parties agree

 9 may be admitted into evidence at any time after opening statements, without the need for a witness
10 to furnish a predicate for their admission. The Parties expressly reserve the right to object to the

11 questioning of a given witness—including the manner of such questioning—about any of the listed

12 exhibits. For this set of documents, “Appendix A” will appear in the column for “sponsoring

13 witness” on the Amended Joint Exhibit List. For the avoidance of doubt, the listing of a sponsoring

14 witness in addition to “Appendix A” for this set of exhibits does not reflect agreement among the

15 Parties that the listed witness may properly be questioned about the exhibit.

16             In filing this Amended Joint Exhibit List, the Parties reserve the right to amend, supplement,
17 or otherwise modify their exhibits and objections if new or additional information is provided at any

18 point. 1

22         •   “FRE 105 (as against [party])” denotes that the party objects to admission of the exhibit
