emails into their document review systems, run the search terms, review for privilege,
and jointly conduct evaluations of sensitive business information for purposes of
confidentiality designation under the Protective Order (given in-house counsel’s
superior position to make such determinations), following which the data
was transmitted to our firm’s discovery platform for responsiveness review and
production.

Mr. Birchall maintain Slack accounts. We have also encountered technical difficulties
with Ms. Zilis's data. We believe those issues have finally been resolved and will
provide that production at the earliest opportunity.

parameters and we are prepared to move forward with the expansion. If certain terms
yield an unmanageably large number of hits, we reserve the right to use Boolean
connectors as appropriate. We can notify you if such modifications are implemented.
