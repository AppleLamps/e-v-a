 1          Pursuant to Civil Local Rule 79-5(f )(3), non-party Shivon Zilis respectfully files this

 2 Response to the OpenAI Defendants’ (“OpenAI’s”) Administrative Motion To Consider Whether

 3 Another Party’s Material Should Be Sealed. Dkt. 272. Ms. Zilis requests limited redactions to

 4 Exhibit E and F (currently provisionally filed under seal at Dkt. 273), which are text strings

 5 between Ms. Zilis and Elon Musk.

 6          Exhibits like these that are “not related, or only tangentially related, to the merits of a case”

 7 are not subject to any strong presumption of public access. Ctr. for Auto Safety v. Chrysler Grp.,

 8 809 F.3d 1092, 1099 (9th Cir. 2016); see also Kamakana v. City & Cnty. of Honolulu, 447 F.3d

 9 1172, 1179 (9th Cir. 2006) (“[T]he public has less of a need for access to court records attached

10 only to non-dispositive motions because those documents are often ‘unrelated, or only tangentially

11 related, to the underlying cause of action.’ ”). Parties moving to seal or redact such documents

12 need only show “good cause” under Rule 26(c). Kamakana, 447 F.3d at 1176. The party must

13 show that “specific prejudice or harm will result” from public filing. Phillips ex rel. Estates of

14 Byrd v. Gen. Motors Corp., 307 F.3d 1206, 1210-11 (9th Cir. 2002).

15          Good cause exists for redactions here. First, Exhibit E discloses Ms. Zilis’s personal

16 phone number on page ZILIS-0002324. Courts routinely permit the redaction of personal phone

17 numbers from public filings. See Epic Games, Inc. v. Apple Inc., No. 4:20-cv-05640, 2021 WL

18 1925460, at *3 (N.D. Cal. Apr. 30, 2021) (Gonzales Rogers, J.) (granting motion to seal that was

19 “narrowly tailored in redacting personal identifying information, including names, phone

20 numbers, and email addresses”); Castro v. ABM Indus., Inc., 325 F.R.D. 332, 334 n.2 (N.D. Cal.

21 2018) (Gonzales Rogers, J.) (finding good cause to seal “names, addresses, and phone numbers”);

22 Am. Auto. Assoc. of N. Cal., Nev. & Utah v. General Motors LLC, No. 17-cv-03874, 2019 WL

23 1206748, at *2 (N.D. Cal. Mar. 14, 2019) (similar). Disclosure of a non-party witness’s personal

24 phone number in a high-profile case like this one raises obvious concerns about invasion of

25 privacy and harassment. Throughout this case, the parties have been redacting personal phone

26 numbers even from sealed filings. But OpenAI inadvertently failed to redact this one instance of

27 Ms. Zilis’s phone number. The Court should permit redaction of the phone number.

 1          Second, Exhibits E and F both contain certain nonresponsive personal content in addition

 2 to their responsive content. Courts permit the redaction of nonresponsive personal content from

 3 documents. See, e.g., United States v. Nosal, No. 08-cr-0237, 2013 WL 11327121, at *9 (N.D.

 4 Cal. Mar. 29, 2013) (permitting redactions to voicemails that “contain[ed] a combination of

 5 personal and business information”); VLSI Tech. LLC v. Intel Corp., No. 17-cv-05671, 2023 WL

 6 7472953, at *2 (N.D. Cal. Oct. 23, 2023) (permitting sealing of “personal information that is not

 7 relevant to any matter in this case”); Apple Inc. v. Rivos, Inc., No. 22-cv-02637, 2024 WL 748394,

 8 at *5 (N.D. Cal. Feb. 23, 2024) (similar). Ms. Zilis requests redaction of the following content:

 9                 Exhibit E, page ZILIS-0002326, text at 2/16/2018, 6:48 AM: redact from
                    after the word “about” to the end of the sentence, and redact the fourth and
10                  fifth sentences in full. As redacted, the text would read: “Will leave you
11                  be on the Demis stuff. I’m sure it’s hard to think about [redacted]. I just
                    needed to say it once since it’s been plaguing me. [redacted].”
12
                   Exhibit F, page ZILIS-0002320, text at 10/23/2022, 1:30 PM: redact from
13                  after the word “above” to before the word “but.” As redacted, the text
                    would read: “No need to call back if you don’t feel like it. I thought it
14                  might help to share some context that appears to be missing above
15                  [redacted] but neither are required.”

16 The redacted material is personal in nature and its disclosure in a public court filing would be an

17 unwarranted invasion of a non-party witness’s privacy.

18          OpenAI has stated that it consents to the redaction of the inadvertently included phone

19 number in Exhibit E and that it has no objection to the redaction of the nonresponsive personal

20 content in Exhibits E and F.
