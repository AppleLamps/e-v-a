 9   III, L.P., OpenAI Startup Fund SPV IV, L.P., Aestas Management Company, LLC, and Aestas

10   LLC (collectively, the “OpenAI Defendants”), and Defendant Microsoft Corporation (“Microsoft,”

11   and together with Plaintiff and the OpenAI Defendants, the “Parties”) submit this Supplement to

12   their Omnibus Sealing Stipulation concerning summary judgment submissions (Dkt. 368).

13           This Supplement concerns Exhibit 59 to the Declaration of Alexandra C. Eynon. The

14   document was originally produced and designated confidential by one of the Parties, and was filed

15   provisionally under seal at Dkt. 353-63. In connection with the Omnibus Sealing Stipulation

16   concerning summary judgment submissions (Dkt. 368), all Parties agreed that the document could

17   be made public, and the document was then publicly filed at Dkt. 379-106. Shortly after the

18   document was publicly filed, a non-party notified the Parties that he asserts a confidentiality interest

19   in certain personal financial information in the exhibit, but because that non-party did not originally

20   produce the document, he had not been notified of the filing and had no prior opportunity to assert

21   his personal confidentiality interest. See Civil Local Rule 79-5(f ).

22           Under the circumstances, the Parties consent to supplementing the Omnibus Sealing

23   Stipulation so that the Court may consider the non-party’s redaction request. The Parties also do

24   not oppose the non-party’s redaction request, while reserving their rights with respect to the use of

25   the information at trial. The publicly filed version of the exhibit at Dkt. 379-106 has been

26   administratively sealed by the Clerk at the non-party’s request pending the Court’s determination

27   of this request.
