11   RESPONSE TO REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 1:

12            Meta objects to Request No. 1 as it seeks documents and communications involving Plaintiffs

13   and Plaintiff-affiliated entities, which are available to the OpenAI Defendants via party discovery. The

15   burden or expense” on non-parties such as Meta, and they have not provided an explanation as to why

16   such Requests are directed at Meta and cannot be sought directly from Plaintiffs. Meta will therefore

17   limit its response to this Request, as further detailed below, to include documents and communications

18   exchanged with the California Attorney General that are otherwise responsive to this Request and not

19   otherwise objected to, but not documents and communications with any Plaintiff or Musk-

20   Affiliated Entity.

21          Meta also objects to this Request as vague, overbroad, and unduly burdensome, because it seeks

22   documents and communications with “any Person or entity” acting on behalf of “any Plaintiff or Musk-

23   Affiliated Entity,” without further information about the identity of such Persons or entities, an

24   explanation as to why other Musk-Affiliated Entities are relevant to this action, or an explanation as to

25   why the burdens of such a request comply with Rule 45(d), which requires the requesting party to “take

26   reasonable steps to avoid imposing undue burden or expense” on the recipient. Meta will therefore limit

27   its response to this Request, as further detailed below, to include documents and communications
