                                  15                    To state a claim for breach of quasi-contract (or “unjust enrichment”),

                                                        sufficiently alleged that the OpenAI defendants unjustly retained the
                                  18                    benefit of their conduct pled in Count II (FAC ¶¶ 260–61, 265), Count
                                                        VI (id. ¶¶ 296–99), and Count VII (id. ¶¶ 305–08, 310–15). Therefore,

                                  15            More recently, courts in this district have explained that even at the pleading stage, “even

                                  16   though a plaintiff can allege multiple theories of recovery, to pursue a quantum meruit claim, ‘a

                                  17   plaintiff may not plead the existence of an enforceable contract and maintain a quasi-contract claim
