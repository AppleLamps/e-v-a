 2          I, Sara Tofighbakhsh, declare as follows:

 3          1.      I am an associate with MoloLamken LLP, counsel for Plaintiffs in Musk, et al. v.

 4 Altman, et al., No. 4:24-cv-04722-YGR (N.D. Cal.). Except as otherwise noted, I have personal

 5 knowledge of the facts stated herein and, if called, could and would testify competently thereto. I

 6 submit this declaration in support of Plaintiffs’ Notice in Response to Discovery Order (Dkt. 314).

 7          2.      Along with another associate and a partner, I participated in filing Plaintiffs’

 8 Administrative Motion to Consider Whether Another Party’s Material Should Be Sealed

 9 (“Administrative Motion,” Dkt. 300) and Discovery Letter Brief (Dkt. 301) (together, the

10 “Motions”). We followed the firm’s standard practice for ECF filings, which requires that two

11 associates file together. One conducts the filing, and a second monitors the filing via screenshare on

12 Microsoft Teams (“Teams”). Both I and the other associate involved in the filing are formerly federal

13 judicial law clerks and highly experienced in ECF filings.

14          3.      At 11:45 p.m. PT on October 8, 2025, I logged into ECF and shared my screen with

15 the other associate over Teams. When I attempted to upload the first exhibit to the Administrative

16 Motion, my file-system window froze. I attempted to upload the exhibit at least two more times, but

17 the problem persisted.

18          4.      As quickly as possible, I arranged for the other associate to log into ECF using a

19 different computer. I monitored the filing via Teams. That associate completed the filing of the

20 Administrative Motion (Dkt. 300) at 12:08 a.m. He then filed the Discovery Letter Brief (Dkt. 301)

21 at 12:20 a.m.

22          5.      I declare under penalty of perjury that the foregoing is true and correct.
