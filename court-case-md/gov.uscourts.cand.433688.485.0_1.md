19          Plaintiff Elon Musk respectfully submits this notice pursuant to this Court’s order in Pretrial
20 Order No. 4 “to file either a notice of voluntary dismissal of [his fraud] claims, or a statement

21 explaining that he elects not to dismiss them.” Dkt. 477 at 4.

22          At the April 17 hearing, Plaintiff proposed to dismiss his fraud and constructive fraud claims
23 (Counts 6 and 7 of the Second Amended Complaint (Dkt. 170) and the Third and Fourth Claims in

24 the proposed jury instructions (Dk. 433)). Plaintiff ’s objective in making his proposal was to

25 streamline the case for trial and to keep the case focused on Plaintiff ’s claims for breach of charitable

26 trust and unjust enrichment – the claims that relate most directly to Plaintiff ’s overriding goal of

27 ensuring that OpenAI adheres to its public charitable mission and obtaining the equitable relief

28 necessary to secure that result.

 1          Plaintiff ’s assumption in making that proposal was that the Court planned to proceed to trial
 2 on the breach of charitable trust and unjust enrichment claims. Plaintiff appreciates that there are

 3 outstanding disputes over the person or entity to whom the proceeds of any disgorgement should be

 4 directed. As Plaintiff explains in his separate verified waiver filed today, that is a question of

 5 equitable remedy that the Court should address at the remedy stage of the case, following any finding

 6 of liability. The Court has many options to ensure that the proceeds of disgorgement benefit

 7 OpenAI’s charitable mission rather than the pecuniary interests of Plaintiff Musk or any other

 8 private party, and it can do so without compromising Musk’s standing to bring his claims.

 9 Accordingly, Musk’s proposal to dismiss his fraud and constructive fraud claims does not depend

10 on how this Court may exercise its equitable discretion to direct the proceeds of any disgorgement

11 during the remedy phase of the case.

12          Thus, so long as the Court plans to proceed to trial on the breach of charitable trust and unjust
13 enrichment claims, Plaintiff respectfully renews his request to dismiss his fraud and constructive

14 fraud claims and confirms that this notice may be treated as a notice of voluntary dismissal of those

15 claims. If the Court plans to dismiss the breach of charitable trust or unjust enrichment claim before

16 trial, Plaintiff respectfully proposes to proceed on his fraud and constructive fraud claims instead.
