                                  13          The Court held a hearing on September 22, 2025 concerning the discovery disputes at ECF

                                  14   Nos. 240, 264 and 273. The Court now issues the following order.

                                  15   A.     ECF Nos. 240 and 264
                                  16          Plaintiffs’ counsel, who are also counsel for non-parties Shivon Zilis and Jared Birchall,

                                  17   have been untimely in producing documents from these subpoena recipients and have not been

                                  18   transparent in their handling of the document productions. OpenAI has made a showing that for

                                  19   the text message productions from Zilis and Musk, at least, something has likely gone

                                  20   significantly wrong. This gives the Court no confidence that Birchall’s text messages were

                                  21   collected or reviewed in an appropriate way. The Court ORDERS Plaintiffs’ counsel to serve one

                                  22   or more detailed declarations explaining the steps they took to comply with the subpoenas to Zilis

                                  23   and Birchall and when they took those steps. This declaration, or these declarations, should

                                  24   explain the technical and logistical challenges counsel encountered. These declarations shall be

                                  25   served within two business days of the date of this order. The Court also ORDERS Plaintiffs’

                                  26   counsel to perform a linear review of all text messages between Zilis and Birchall, or between

                                  27   either of them and Musk. A “linear review” means that counsel must look at each text message to

                                  28   determine if it is relevant and responsive and cannot, for example, rely on search terms.

                                  17   C.     Where This Leaves The Parties
                                  18          For phase I discovery, the Court thinks that the October 1, 2025 deadline to complete fact

                                  19   depositions is the close of fact discovery within the meaning of Civil Local Rule 37-3. That

                                  20   means that October 8, 2025 is the deadline for all discovery-related motions.1 As the commentary

                                  21   to the rule observes, “‘Discovery-related motions’ encompasses all motions relating to discovery,

                                  22   including motions to compel or protect against discovery, motions regarding the duty to preserve

                                  23   documents, including spoliation motions, motions to quash or enforce subpoenas, and motions for

                                  24   discovery sanctions.” Pursuant to the undersigned’s Discovery Standing Order, motions to

                                  25   compel must be brought in a joint discovery letter brief. By contrast, a sanctions motion must be
