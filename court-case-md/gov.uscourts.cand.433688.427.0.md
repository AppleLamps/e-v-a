12 I.        THE ACTION
13           A.      Substance of the Action
14           Plaintiff Elon Musk asserts claims against Sam Altman, Greg Brockman, the OpenAI
15 nonprofit, and other OpenAI entities for breach of charitable trust, unjust enrichment, fraud, and

16 constructive fraud; and against Microsoft for aiding and abetting breach of charitable trust. The

17 claims arise out of charitable contributions Musk caused to be made to the OpenAI nonprofit from

18 2016 to 2020, and the OpenAI nonprofit’s creation and operation of a for-profit affiliate.

19           In 2015, Musk, Altman, Brockman, and Ilya Sutskever founded a nonprofit organization
20 called OpenAI, Inc. (now called The OpenAI Foundation) with the purpose of ensuring that artificial

21 general intelligence benefits all of humanity. Musk provided approximately $38 million in financial

22 support to the OpenAI nonprofit and also provided various forms of non-monetary support. Musk

23 resigned from the OpenAI nonprofit’s board in February 2018.

24           In September 2018, the OpenAI nonprofit created a for-profit subsidiary to which it later
25 transferred substantially all of its intellectual property in return for an interest in the for-profit entity.

26 Most of the individuals who had been employed by the nonprofit became employees of the for-

27 profit subsidiary. Investors in the for-profit entity, including the OpenAI nonprofit, were entitled to

28 earn returns up to specified profit “caps.” The OpenAI nonprofit also received an uncapped residual

 9          Plaintiff asserts that Altman, Brockman, and the OpenAI nonprofit are liable for breach of

10 charitable trust because Musk made contributions to the OpenAI nonprofit for charitable purposes,

11 but the OpenAI nonprofit then used the contributions for unauthorized commercial purposes.

12 Plaintiff claims that Altman, Brockman, the OpenAI nonprofit, and the other OpenAI entities named

13 as defendants are liable for unjust enrichment for similar reasons. Plaintiff further claims that

14 Altman, Brockman, and the OpenAI nonprofit are liable for fraud and constructive fraud because

15 they made false statements to Musk about whether they intended for OpenAI to remain a nonprofit

16 and thereby misled Musk into continuing to contribute to the nonprofit. Finally, Plaintiff asserts

17 that Microsoft aided and abetted OpenAI’s breach of charitable trust by knowingly providing

18 substantial assistance to OpenAI’s alleged breach of charitable trust.

19          The OpenAI Defendants deny Musk’s claims. As for the charitable trust claim, the OpenAI

20 Defendants dispute that any of Musk’s contributions created an enforceable charitable trust, and

21 they further dispute that they used Musk’s contributions for any unauthorized purpose. As for the

22 fraud and constructive fraud claims, the OpenAI Defendants dispute that any OpenAI Defendant

23 made any actionable statement, that any statement Musk has identified was false or misleading, or

24 that Musk actually or reasonably relied on any such statement. As for Musk’s unjust enrichment

25 claim, the OpenAI Defendants dispute that any OpenAI Defendant unjustly retained any benefit at

26 Musk’s expense, and they also dispute the existence and terms of any implied agreement that could

27 support quasi-contract relief. In addition, as affirmative defenses, the OpenAI Defendants contend

28 that Musk’s claims are barred in whole or in substantial part by the applicable statutes of limitations
