               1              I, Michael Wetter, state and declare as follows:

               2              1.     I joined Microsoft Corporation (“Microsoft”) as an employee in March 2007.

               3              2.     My current role at Microsoft is Corporate Vice President of Corporate Development.

               4              3.     As Corporate Vice President of Corporate Development, I am responsible for

               5     engaging with external partners and stakeholders to facilitate acquisition and investment processes

               6     and discussions, including coordinating access to diligence materials, drafting and reviewing

               7     agreements, and participating in meetings to discuss acquisition and investment terms, and

               8     financial plans and analysis.

               9              4.     I submit this Declaration in support of Microsoft’s Opposition to Plaintiffs’ Motion
              10     for a Preliminary Injunction. I have personal knowledge of all the facts stated herein, and if called

              11     as a witness could and would competently testify to them under oath.

              12     October 2024 Funding Round for OpenAI

              13              5.     As part of my role as Corporate Vice President of Corporate Development, I was

              14     deeply involved in the deal negotiations with OpenAI related to its October 2024 funding round

              15     and am familiar with the terms and conditions of investment that were negotiated with OpenAI.

              16              6.     At no point during negotiations for the October 2024 funding round did anyone at

              17     OpenAI ask me to agree that Microsoft would not invest in OpenAI’s competitors. I am also not

              18     aware of anyone else at Microsoft being asked to agree to such terms.

              19              7.     At no point during negotiations for the October 2024 funding round did I suggest to
              20     anyone at OpenAI, or anyone else, that OpenAI condition other investors’ participation on an

              21     agreement not to invest in OpenAI’s competitors. I am also not aware of anyone else at Microsoft

              22     suggesting that.

              23              8.     At no point during negotiations for the October 2024 funding round did anyone at

              24     OpenAI discuss with me whether either OpenAI or Microsoft would ask other investors to agree

              25     to not invest in OpenAI’s competitors. I am also not aware of anyone at OpenAI suggesting that

              26     to anyone else at Microsoft.

              27              9.     Had OpenAI, or anyone else, asked me to abstain from investing in other AI

              28     companies, I would not have agreed to those terms. To the best of my knowledge, had anyone at
