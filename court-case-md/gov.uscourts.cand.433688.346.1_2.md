 3          1.      I am a partner with MoloLamken LLP, counsel for Plaintiffs in Musk, et al. v. Altman,

 6 Plaintiffs’ Response in Opposition to Non-Party Ilya Sutskever’s Motion to Stay.

11          3.      On October 17, 2025, this Court “ORDER[ED] (1) Sutskever to produce the

12 Brockman memo to Plaintiffs and (2) Plaintiffs may depose Sutskever in a second deposition

13 concerning the value of his financial interest in OpenAI and concerning the Brockman memo.” Dkt.

14 No. 324.

15          4.      Over the next eleven days, I attempted to negotiate compliance with counsel for Mr.

16 Sutskever, whose fees are being paid by OpenAI. On the morning of October 18, I requested that

17 Mr. Sutskever comply with the Order by providing the Brockman Memo and dates for a follow-up

18 deposition. Mr. Sutskever’s counsel responded that they intended to appeal the Order. On October

19 20, I requested that Mr. Sutskever comply promptly with this Court’s order notwithstanding any
20 appeal, consistent with the law in this Circuit.

21          5.      The parties then met and conferred in good faith. Mr. Sutskever’s counsel twice

22 proposed: (1) to make the Brockman Memo available for inspection only, under highly restrictive

23 procedures not required by law; and (2) to make Mr. Sutskever available for a video deposition

24 regarding only the value of his equity stake in OpenAI, not regarding the Brockman Memo.

25          6.      On October 23, Plaintiffs’ counsel agreed to accept the Brockman Memo subject to

26 the highly restrictive procedures demanded by Mr. Sutskever’s counsel and to depose Mr. Sutskever

27 remotely. Plaintiffs’ counsel also insisted that Mr. Sutskever comply with the Order and answer
