                                  10          In ECF No. 300, Plaintiffs filed a motion to consider whether other parties’ materials

                                  11   should be sealed. The motion concerned Exhibit 1, the joint discovery letter brief, portions of

                                  12   which contained information that Defendants and non-party Ilya Sutskever designated as
Northern District of California
 United States District Court




                                  13   confidential. The motion also concerned two meet and confer correspondences, Exhibits 2 and 3,

                                  14   that Sutskever designated as confidential. Finally, the motion addressed Exhibit 4, an excerpt

                                  15   from Sutskever’s deposition. OpenAI and Sutskever filed responses at ECF Nos. 333 and 334.

                                  16   OpenAI requests three redactions to the deposition transcript. Those proposed redactions are

                                  17   appropriate. Sutskever proposes a single redaction to page 3 of the joint discovery letter brief, and

                                  18   a ton of redactions to the deposition transcript. Sutskever’s proposed redactions (beyond what

                                  19   OpenAI has proposed) fail the standards for a motion to seal. Accordingly, the Court ORDERS

                                  20   Plaintiffs to file the joint discovery letter brief in the public record with no redactions within five

                                  21   days. The Court ORDERS OpenAI to file the deposition excerpt in the public record with only

                                  22   the redactions that OpenAI has proposed, and to do this within five days. As OpenAI and

                                  23   Sutskever have not requested sealing of the meet and confer exchanges (Exhibits 2 and 3), the

                                  24   Court ORDERS Plaintiffs to file those in the public record within five days.

                                  25          IT IS SO ORDERED.
