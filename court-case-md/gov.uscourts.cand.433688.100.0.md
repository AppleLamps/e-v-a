11          WHEREAS, on August 5, 2024, Plaintiff Musk filed a Complaint against the OpenAI

12 Defendants (ECF No. 1);

13          WHEREAS, on October 8, 2024, the OpenAI Defendants filed their Motion to Dismiss the

14 Complaint (ECF No. 25);

15          WHEREAS, on October 15, 2024, the Court granted Plaintiff Musk’s and the OpenAI

16 Defendants’ stipulation extending the deadline for Plaintiff Musk to respond to the Motion to

17 Dismiss by filing an Opposition or amending the Complaint to November 14, 2024 (ECF No. 29);

18          WHEREAS, on November 14, 2024, Plaintiffs filed a 105-page First Amended Complaint,

19 asserting twenty-six claims (ECF No. 32);

20          WHEREAS, on November 19, 2024, the Court entered an Order setting the deadline to

21 respond to the First Amended Complaint for December 10, 2024 (ECF No. 38);

22          WHEREAS, on December 5, 2024, the Court entered an Order granting the Parties’

23 stipulation extending the deadline for Defendants to respond to the First Amended Complaint to

24 January 31, 2025, and setting the deadline for Plaintiffs to oppose any motion(s) to dismiss the

25 First Amended Complaint to March 17, 2025, and the deadline to file any reply(ies) in support of

26 any motion(s) to dismiss the First Amended Complaint to April 18, 2025 (ECF No. 61);

27          WHEREAS, in light of the number of claims asserted in Plaintiffs’ First Amended

28 Complaint, Plaintiffs and the OpenAI Defendants have agreed, subject to the Court’s approval, to
