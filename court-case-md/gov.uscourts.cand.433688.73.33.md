 1                                         DECLARATION

 2          I, Antonio Gracias, declare as follows:

 3          1.      I am the Founder, Chief Executive Officer, and Chief Investment Officer of

 4 Valor Equity Partners (“Valor”), a venture capital growth fund with approximately $25 billion

 5 in gross assets. I submit this declaration in support of Plaintiffs’ Motion for a Preliminary

 6 Injunction. I have personal knowledge of the facts stated in this declaration, except as

 7 otherwise noted, and if called as a witness, I could and would competently testify to them.

 8          2.      I am informed that in October 2024, OpenAI conducted an oversubscribed

 9 funding round that valued the company at $157 billion and set minimum subscriptions at $250

10 million. In my experience, only a very small group of investors, including Valor, are capable

11 of investment at this level, and the principals in this investment community know each other,

12 have professional relationships and frequently communicate.

13          3.      I can attest that it was common knowledge in and among this close-knit

14 investment community that OpenAI, during its oversubscribed October 2024 funding round,

15 imposed as a condition to any investment allocation that investors agree not to fund OpenAI’s

16 competitors, including xAI, which it specifically named among others.

17          4.      The statements of Sam Altman, during his December 4, 2024 interview with

18 Andrew Ross Sorkin at the 2024 New York Times Dealbook Summit, to the effect that OpenAI

19 solely imposed restrictions on the information rights of those of its investors which also

20 invested in competitors, are not consistent with my information and belief regarding OpenAI’s
21 more restrictive non-compete understanding with investors in its October 2024 round.

22          5.      In my judgment, this arrangement naturally encourages coordinated behavior

23 and mutual reinforcement among the dozen investors receiving an allocation in OpenAI's

24 October 2024 funding round to collectively adhere to OpenAI's investment restriction,

25 because if some investors do and others do not, those who adhere and fail to diversify their AI

26 investments are at a competitive disadvantage.

27          6.      In addition, investors bound exclusively to OpenAI are disproportionately

28 exposed to the vicissitudes of its performance, while peers could hedge through diversification
