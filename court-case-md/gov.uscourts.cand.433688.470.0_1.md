 1          Plaintiff Elon Musk; Defendants Samuel Altman, Gregory Brockman, OpenAI, Inc., et al.
 2 (collectively, the “OpenAI Defendants”); and Defendant Microsoft Corporation (collectively with

 3 Plaintiff and the OpenAI Defendants, the “Parties”), hereby stipulate as follows:

 4          WHEREAS, Pretrial Order No. 1 (Dkt. 446) requires the Parties to lodge deposition
 5 transcripts that may be used at trial with the Court by Friday, April 17, 2026;

 6          WHEREAS, Pretrial Order No. 1 further requires that lodged deposition transcripts shall be
 7 signed original copies or a certified/stipulated copy if, for any reason, the original is not available;

 8          NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties stipulate that the certified deposition transcripts and any
 9 errata sheets being lodged with the Court may be used at trial.
