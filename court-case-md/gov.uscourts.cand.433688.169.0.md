 1   I.      PURPOSES AND LIMITATIONS

 2           1.1       Disclosures and discovery in this action are likely to involve production of

 3   confidential, proprietary, or private information for which special protection from public disclosure

 4   and from use for any purpose other than prosecuting this litigation may be warranted. Accordingly,

 5   the Parties hereby stipulate to and respectfully request the Court to enter this Stipulated Protective

 6   Order. The Parties acknowledge that this Stipulated Protective Order does not confer blanket

 7   protections on all disclosures or responses to discovery and that the protection it affords from public

 8   disclosure and use extends only to the limited information or items that are entitled to confidential

 9   treatment under the applicable legal principles. The Parties further acknowledge, as set forth in

10   Paragraph 12.3, below, that this Stipulated Protective Order does not entitle them to file confidential

11   information under seal; Civil Local Rule 79-5 sets forth the procedures that must be followed and

12   the standards that will be applied when a party seeks permission from the court to file material under

13   seal.

18   stored or maintained) that a Producing Party in good faith and reasonably believes contains

20   otherwise qualifies for protection under Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 26(c).

21           2.3          “Counsel” means Outside Counsel of Record and House Counsel (as well as their

22   support staff).

26   manner in which they are generated, stored, or maintained (including, among other things, testimony,

12   information copied or extracted from Protected Material; (c) all copies, excerpts, summaries, or

23   protected information; and (c) any use of Protected Material at trial, which shall be governed by a

 3          5.1           Restraint and Care in Designating Protected Material. Each Party or Non-Party

 8   protection is not warranted are not swept unjustifiably within the ambit of this Order. Mass,

10   unjustified or made for an improper purpose (e.g., to cause unnecessary delay, expense, or burdens)

 1   VIII. PROTECTED MATERIAL SUBPOENAED OR ORDERED PRODUCED IN OTHER

 2          PROCEEDINGS

 3          8.1        If a Receiving Party is served with a subpoena or a court order issued in another

 4   proceeding that compels disclosure of Protected Material, that Party must: (a) promptly notify the

 5   Designating Party in writing, which shall include a copy of the subpoena or court order; (b)

 6   promptly notify in writing the party who caused the subpoena or order to issue in the other

 7   proceeding that some or all of the material sought by the subpoena or order is subject to this

 8   Protective Order, which notice shall include a copy of this Stipulated Protective Order; and (c)

 9   cooperate with all reasonable procedures sought to be pursued by the Designating Party whose

10   Protected Material may be affected.

11          8.2        If the Designating Party timely seeks a protective order in the other proceeding, the

12   Party served with the subpoena or court order shall not produce any Protected Material before a

13   determination by the court from which the subpoena or order issued, unless the Party has obtained
