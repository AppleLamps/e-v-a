 1          Pursuant to Civil Local Rule 7-11 and Section 3 of this Court’s Standing Order in Civil
 2 Cases, Plaintiffs Elon Musk and X.AI Corp. (the “Plaintiffs”) respectfully file this motion for
 3 administrative relief to modify the Case Management and Pretrial Order by extending the
 4 deadlines for expert discovery by 35 days as set forth below. Good cause for this extension exists
 5 for the following reasons.
 6          This dispute arises out of Plaintiffs’ claims that Defendants breached their obligations to
 7 Plaintiff Elon Musk by improperly transforming OpenAI into a for-profit enterprise after Musk
 8 contributed tens of millions of dollars to OpenAI on the understanding that it would remain a
 9 nonprofit dedicated to the public good. On April 9, 2025, this Court entered an expedited
10 schedule for the trial of certain Phase I claims. Dkt. 146. On July 25, 2025, the Court extended

11 that schedule by stipulation. Dkts. 214, 215. Pursuant to that expedited schedule, the parties are
12 currently in the midst of conducting 16 fact depositions in the span of 30 days. Fact discovery is
13 scheduled to close on October 1, 2025. Id. Opening expert reports are due a mere fourteen days
14 later on October 15, 2025. Id.
15          Recent developments have made that highly-compressed schedule even more challenging
16 and difficult to meet than it already is. Plaintiffs’ core claim for breach of charitable trust depends
17 on a complete picture of OpenAI’s for-profit structure and finances, and Plaintiffs’ claim for
18 disgorgement of wrongful profits relies heavily on Defendants’ own financial forecasts and
19 valuations. Defendant OpenAI, however, initially refused to produce financial information in
20 response to Plaintiffs’ discovery requests. On July 9, 2025, Magistrate Judge Hixson granted
21 Plaintiffs’ motion to compel production of that information. Dkt. 203 at 4. Nonetheless, OpenAI
22 continued to withhold critical financial information under the so-called “business strategy
23 doctrine.” On September 9, 2025, Judge Hixson granted Plaintiffs’ second motion to compel.
24 Dkt. 251.
25          OpenAI finally produced the information the evening of September 14, 2025. OpenAI’s
26 production includes crucial forecasts, valuations, and other financial analysis that Plaintiffs’
27 damages expert will consider when formulating his opinions. Plaintiffs were deprived of that
28 information until late in the discovery process due to OpenAI’s discovery positions that Judge

 3          Further complicating matters, on September 11, 2025, OpenAI and Microsoft publicly
 4 announced a Memorandum of Understanding that would substantially alter OpenAI’s corporate
 5 structure. OpenAI produced information about those plans in its September 14 production. This
 6 development is also highly material to multiple expert reports that Plaintiffs plan to submit.
 7          Finally, Plaintiffs originally noticed OpenAI’s Rule 30(b)(6) deposition for September 18,
 8 2025, but that deposition was rescheduled at OpenAI’s request to October 1, 2025, due to witness
 9 unavailability. Plaintiffs anticipate that OpenAI’s corporate representative will provide crucial
10 testimony on multiple topics. But because Plaintiffs accommodated OpenAI’s scheduling request,

11 Plaintiffs will not obtain that testimony until the very last day of fact discovery.
12          Plaintiffs’ experts plan to address a number of complex topics, such as AI safety, standards
13 and practices for nonprofits, and the wrongful profits that Defendants have derived from Musk’s
14 contributions. AI is an emerging technology, and financial projections and valuations for AI
15 ventures present unusual challenges. It would disserve the interests of justice to require experts to
16 prepare and submit reports on these complex topics a mere two weeks after the close of fact
17 discovery given the above recent developments.
18          A five-week extension of expert discovery would better accommodate the substantial work
19 the parties’ experts confront. That extension will not prejudice the parties or the Court. Even as
20 extended, expert discovery will conclude on January 9, 2026, six weeks before the existing
21 compliance deadline of February 20, 2026, and long before the existing trial date of March 30,
22 2026. While Defendants have expressed concerns about their experts’ availability, Plaintiffs are
23 willing to work cooperatively to find convenient dates on which all experts may be deposed.1

 1                                            DECLARATION
 2          MARC TOBEROFF declares as follows pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1746:
 3          1.      I represent Plaintiffs Elon Musk and X.AI Corp. and submit this declaration
 4 pursuant to Civil Local Rule 7-11(a) to explain why a stipulation could not be obtained in
 5 this matter.
 6          2.      On September 10, 2025, I wrote to opposing counsel to request a stipulation for a
 7 four to five week extension of expert discovery. I observed that under the current circumstances it
 8 would be quite difficult for any expert to be able to adequately review the full set of facts in this
 9 case and apply them in this novel context in just two weeks after the close of fact discovery.
10          3.      On September 14, 2025, opposing counsel responded that they did not see any

11 reason to modify the expert report deadlines because Defendants and their experts had relied on
12 the existing deadlines in their case planning, including to ensure availability during the current
13 expert deposition window. Defendants stated that they would not oppose an application to move
14 the deadlines for submitting opening and rebuttal expert reports and for completing expert
15 discovery by one week.

17          I declare under penalty of perjury that the foregoing is true and correct.
