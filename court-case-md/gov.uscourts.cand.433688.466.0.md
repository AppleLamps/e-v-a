 1          On March 31, 2026, Monica Veleber (“Movant”) filed a one-page document styled as an

 2   “Emergency Motion to Intervene.” Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 24 governs intervention.

 3   Movant’s filing does not satisfy any requirement for intervention under Fed. R. Civ. P. 24. It

 4   does not attempt to establish entitlement to intervention as of right under Rule 24(a), nor does it

 5   meet the standard for permissive intervention under Rule 24(b). Further, intervention at this late

 6   stage would unduly delay and prejudice the adjudication of the rights of the parties to this

 7   litigation. See Fed. R. Civ. P. 24(b)(3). The filing also fails to set out the claim(s) or defense(s)

 8   for which intervention is sought as required by Rule 24(c). The motion should be denied.
