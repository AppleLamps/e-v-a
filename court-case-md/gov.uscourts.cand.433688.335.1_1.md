 1                                             DECLARATION

 2          I, Jennifer Schubert, declare as follows:

 3          1.      I am a partner with MoloLamken LLP, counsel for Plaintiffs in Musk, et al. v. Altman,

 4 et al., No. 4:24-cv-04722-YGR (N.D. Cal.). I have personal knowledge of the facts stated herein

 5 and, if called, could and would testify competently thereto. I submit this declaration in support of

 6 Plaintiffs’ Application for an Order to Show Cause.

 7          2.      On October 17, 2025, this Court “ORDER[ED] (1) Sutskever to produce the

 8 Brockman memo to Plaintiffs and (2) Plaintiffs may depose Sutskever in a second deposition

 9 concerning the value of his financial interest in OpenAI and concerning the Brockman memo.” Dkt.
10 No. 324.

11          3.      On October 18, I requested that pursuant to the Order, counsel for Mr. Sutskever

12 provide Plaintiffs with the Brockman Memo and Mr. Sutskever’s availability to sit for a follow-up

13 deposition in San Francisco. See Ex. A. Mr. Sutskever’s counsel responded that they intended to

14 appeal the Order. Id. On October 20, Plaintiffs’ counsel requested prompt compliance with this

15 Court’s order even pending appeal.

16          4.      On the evening of October 20, 2025, I conferred with Mr. Sutskever’s counsel by

17 phone. Mr. Sutskever’s counsel offered not to appeal if Plaintiffs would accept a compromise

18 proposal. Specifically, Mr. Sutskever’s counsel offered (1) to make the Brockman Memo available

19 for inspection under highly restrictive procedures, rather than producing it under the protective order;

20 and (2) to make Mr. Sutskever available for a video deposition but only regarding the value of his

21 equity stake in OpenAI and not regarding the Brockman memo. On that call, I reiterated that Mr.

22 Sutskever was required to comply with the Order promptly, notwithstanding his plans to appeal. Mr.

23 Sutskever’s counsel responded that they did not intend to supply the discovery pending appeal.

24          5.      On October 23, I again conferred with Mr. Sutskever’s counsel. Plaintiffs’ counsel

25 agreed to depose Mr. Sutskever remotely using standard remote deposition procedures, including

26 that he be alone in the room during his deposition, and to accept the Brockman Memo under the

27 highly restrictive procedures insisted upon by Mr. Sutskever’s counsel. I also insisted that Mr.

 8 review,” citing two cases, Alvarez v. Larose, No. 320-CV-00782DMSAHG, 2020 WL 5632659, at

 9 *2 (S.D. Cal. Sept. 21, 2020), and Jones v. PGA Tour, Inc., No. 22-CV-04486-BLF, 2023 WL
10 2541326, at *2 (N.D. Cal. Mar. 15, 2023). In both cases, the parties who declined to comply with

11 the court’s order sought a stay. As of the time of this filing, Mr. Sutskever has not filed for a stay,

12 nor has he filed an appeal.

13          7.      Plaintiffs are scheduled to depose Mira Murati on November 2, 2025. We intend to

15 response to defendants’ motions for summary judgment is due on November 7. The evidence

16 adduced from the Brockman Memo and Mr. Sutskever’s deposition will be relevant to our brief and

17 may be relevant to our experts’ opinions.
