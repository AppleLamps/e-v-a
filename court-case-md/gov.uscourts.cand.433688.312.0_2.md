                                  13   witness Ilya Sutskever. The Court DENIES the motion because it is untimely under Civil Local

                                  14   Rule 37-3. The close of fact discovery was October 1, 2025, and therefore the deadline to file any

                                  15   discovery-related motions concerning fact discovery was October 8, 2025. The Court previously

                                  16   explained that to the parties. See ECF No. 276 at 2-3. And it is clear that Plaintiffs knew that was

                                  17   the deadline. ECF No. 302-2 (Plaintiffs’ counsel: “As a party to the scheduling order in the case,

                                  18   we do have to file tonight under the rules because it’s 7 days after the close of fact discovery.

                                  19   Magistrate Judge Hixson does not have authority to extend that deadline.”). Plaintiffs were correct

                                  20   about what their deadline was. However, they missed the deadline because they filed after

                                  21   midnight, so the filing was actually made on October 9, 2025. Civ. L.R. 5-1(d)(4) (“All electronic

                                  22   filings of documents must be completed as described in Civil L.R. 5-1(d)(3) prior to midnight in

                                  23   order to be considered timely filed that day.”). Accordingly, their motion is DENIED.
