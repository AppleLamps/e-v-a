16   corporation has a financial interest in OpenAI OpCo, L.L.C. of 10% or more. OpenAI OpCo,
17   L.L.C. states that Microsoft Corporation is a publicly held corporation that has a financial interest
18   in OpenAI Global LLC of 10% or more.

20   publicly held corporation has a financial interest in OpenAI, L.L.C. of 10% or more. OpenAI
21   L.L.C. further states that it is an indirect subsidiary of OpenAI Global, LLC, and Microsoft
22   Corporation is a publicly held corporation that has a financial interest in OpenAI Global LLC of
23   10% or more.
24          4.      OpenAI GP, L.L.C. states that it is a subsidiary of OpenAI Group PBC and that no
25   publicly held corporation has a financial interest in OpenAI GP, L.L.C. of 10% or more.
26          5.      OpenAI Global, LLC states that it is a subsidiary of OAI Corporation and OpenAI
27   Global HoldCo, Inc. and that Microsoft Corporation is a publicly held corporation that has a
28   financial interest in OpenAI Global, LLC of 10% or more.
