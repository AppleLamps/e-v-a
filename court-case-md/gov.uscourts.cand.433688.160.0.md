 1          Leave to file an amicus brief will be granted where the brief would “assist [the] court” in

 2   resolving the matter at issue by “supplementing the efforts of counsel [or] drawing the court’s

 3   attention to law that escaped consideration.” Cody v. Ring LLC, 718 F. Supp. 3d 993, 1004 (N.D.

 4   Cal. 2024) (quoting Miller-Wohl Co. v. Comm’r of Labor & Indus. State of Mont., 694 F.2d 203,

 5   204 (9th Cir. 1982)).

 6          The OpenAI Defendants do not object to the filing of the former employees’ proposed

 7   amicus brief. That said, the proposed brief does not “assist [the] court” in resolving the OpenAI

 8   Defendants’ pending motion to dismiss, nor “draw[] the court’s attention to law that escaped

 9   consideration” during prior, and now completed, briefing. Id. The proposed brief consists of factual

10   assertions and personal opinions and cites no legal authority. It has no bearing on the legal

11   sufficiency of the allegations in Plaintiffs’ First Amended Complaint.

12          Confirming this, the brief says nothing in support of Plaintiffs’ primary allegations of

13   breach. Musk alleges that in 2019 OpenAI formed a for-profit entity, OpenAI, L.P, “to siphon assets

14   from the non-profit” in violation of OpenAI’s charitable mission. Dkt. 128 at 2-3. By contrast,

15   proposed amici emphasize that this exact structure—“a 501(c)(3) public charity” that “controls

16   several for-profit subsidiaries”—was “a crucial part” of OpenAI’s “overall strategy,” “critical to

17   OpenAI’s ability to attract and retain talent,” and a “fundamental safeguard ensuring OpenAI would

18   never stray” from its charitable mission. Dkt. 154-1 at 1, 2, 5, 11.

19          Proposed amici thus identify no past or present “breach” or harm to Musk or anyone else

20   flowing from OpenAI’s existing structure, which has been in place for over six years. Rather,

21   proposed amici argue that a “violation” of OpenAI’s “mission” may occur in the future if OpenAI

22   were to “change . . . [its] corporate structure” to “remove[] the Nonprofit’s controlling role.” Id. at

23   2, 10-11. Proposed amici’s views on a hypothetical organizational change—that has not yet

24   occurred and may never occur—provide no support for, and are irrelevant to, Musk’s allegations

25   that OpenAI long ago breached purported commitments to him. In any event, OpenAI has made

26   clear that the nonprofit will remain in place under any contemplated restructuring, and recently

27   announced the formation of a NonProfit Commission to inform the organization’s philanthropic
